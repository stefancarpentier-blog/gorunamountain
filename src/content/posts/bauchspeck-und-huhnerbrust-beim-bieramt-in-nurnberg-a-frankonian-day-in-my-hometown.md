I managed to meet up with my kinda long term friend Oliver this week. I mean loooong term. Since 7th grade long. When we meet, it's like no time has passed at all. We have pretty different lives, but we align well, somehow, every time and instantly. After building himself and his wife a house ~50km south of Nuernberg he seems really content with the life they built themselves together. We went from one place in town to the next, drinking some great coffee, beer, get some food and exchange stories and experiences. What a treat. One of the highlights was to sit on the cobblestone plaza next to the Duerer House and have some beer, sitting on the street with 100-200 others, similar minded people. The place selling the delicious selection of beer there is called "Bier Amt" translated "department of beer". It's a truly unique setting where they decided to have it, they employ unique characters of the Nuernberg scene. Friendly and welcoming, but with a fast whit I have not heard in a while. Oliver asked what type of finger-food they would have for us. Looking around there was no food to be seen. The bar-keeper was quick with a reply. I can offer you some "Bauchspeck and Huehnerbrust" (belly fat and chicken breast) pointing to the belly of his partner behind the counter and then squeezing his own breast. It took me a second. Everybody had a great laugh, we walked away without food today but with great beer and a smile.

### Marita is getting back on the bike

Last year my Mom got herself a new Bicycle. At the age of 81. Congratulation. The only bad thing was that the ground came closer than wanted and faster than wanted in one of the 1st rides with the new beauty. Needless to say, the bike stood in the garage most of the time since then. I took out my training hat and we did 3 trips so far training and getting the confidence back that was with her not too long ago. It's hard work but she seems to make great progress. Riding slow, on singletrack, one-handed, emergency braking. All the lectures you need to get back on that horse. Good job Mom.

### Getting ready to ride to Apulia

The next phase of my trip will be on two wheels. Riding from Nuernberg to Apulia, or stopping early if I feel like it. The route is planned (~2100km) and I will have two one day companions. 1st day Kurt will come along and on day two of crossing the alps, Klaus will join me for a day. I am super excited and happy I will have 2 days of company along the way. I will bring a total of 8kg of stuff with me. A strap-on saddlebag and a little backpack.
