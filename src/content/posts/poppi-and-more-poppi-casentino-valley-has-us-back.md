It's been a long time since my last post. But I made it to Poppi. It is the area we are looking to buy a property.

The temperatures climbing out of the Poo delta, over the Apennines and into the Casentino valley were really warm, but nothing like it was in the plains. I started my ride at 6.00 and it was still ~25C in the area. My navigation APP went CRAZY and guided me north for a while, till I finally pulled the plug and went into manual navigation mode. ==> not all were the best suitable cycling roads and the elevation I rode was more than prescribed. BUT but but. It was beautiful in the area. As soon as I climbed out of the plains, trees started to appear and cars thinned out as I used smaller roads crossing over ridges to get to the roads less traveled. All this search ended up "costing" me 80m extra climbing. These were such beautiful climbs, I would do them again in a heartbeat. The scenery was wide and remote, lots of forests and I was in heaven. To get up Passa Calla was a bit of a workout, but 1/2 liter of wine together with 1.5 liters of water at a little tourist resort gave me the needed patt on the butt to make it up there. A little tipsy but very happy I go to start my descent into the Casentino Valley. 17km of straight and beautiful downhill through the amazing forest, partially part of the National Park of the area. Stia awaited me and with that known territory and streets. After a total of 9 hours, I arrived in Francesco and Lucrezia's place. The timing could not have been better as I arrived just when they started lunch. Heaven was right here. The following morning Francesco got to experience what it looks like when a hungry Stefan eats breakfast. I think I scared him for life. As the dinner the night before was really tiny, I took the liberty to go to town. I think Francesco saw a side of me he has not yet seen. I was the food inhaling monster I tend to be after a hard ride.

Though I wanted to only take 1-2 days rest here and then move on to Apulia, all the way in the south of Italy, I decided it is too hot to move on. I also thought it would be wise to spend some good time in the area we are so much looking forward to buying a house and property. In the mean-time, I helped out Francesco with his BnB and with the party as well as the wedding of Abi and Lorela. A young and well-grounded as well as super lively and diverse couple. He is Brazilian-Japanese and she is Italian-??? living in Sydney and currently in the process of moving back into the Casentino Valley. I helped to serve them, doing this and that, and in the end, we all danced till we could no longer dance. 5am was my final call, but I heard of people going on till 9.30. The following day, all met back up at the pool and tried to get rid of the hang-over. I had so much water, I was fine :-).

After 1 1/2 weeks, I picked up Mumu at Florence airport after getting a rental car. We got our heads together, looked at more property, AGAIN. and discussed and pretended to sleep while thinking all night and more of the same and more..... I think we finally made up our mind to try to spell out an offer for one property. It is the biggest land of all, it is the furthest away from any town and it is the prettiest house we have looked at.
