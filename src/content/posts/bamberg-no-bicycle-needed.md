#### So we were in Bamberg,

a small town 60km north of Nuernberg. It is not super well known, but a good amount of tourists and locals from Frankonia make their way to this Gem of an old, stylish, livable and human-sized town.

It has three major waterways through it. The Main, Regnitz and then there is the "Rhein-Main-Donau Kanal".  The center of town is sliced up nicely by the water and it makes for a truly great experience for a walk through town. You always seem to have a great view of some part of town, involving water.

![Bamberg-3waterways](/photos/bamberg-3waterways.png)

### Bamberg - no bicycle needed

We ended up "stranded" in a nice coffee shop, run by a German-Italian guy (born in Germany, living most of his life in Rome and northern Italy) he decided a few years ago to settle in Bamberg and roast his beans and brew some awesome coffee for his customers. No "TO GO" cups are coming from him, ever. He is opposed to the runaway culture that spilled into the coffee scene thanks to Starbucks. No good words from him about that trend as you can imagine. We ended up talking to him a while in-between his runs to serve coffee and clean tables. He decided to live in Bamberg and still prefers it to Nuernberg because of its size. Nuernberg, it's so big, he said. You need a bicycle to get around; Nono, that is not good for him. In Bamberg, you can walk everywhere and find everything you need and want. I had to laugh, but I also had to agree. It is a very charming town and is even more human size than Nuernberg is. I just found it so amazing to be able to compare between these two modes of transportation, car not even being an option. No Car ! No Car ! No Car ! No Car ! No Car ! No Car ! No Car ! No Car !
