I am just now reading this sourdough book from Sarah Owens in German. In the first few pages I find an amazing quote that I need to share.

# How can you call a nation Grand, when their bread tastes like Kleenex?

Think about that for a while. It's of course for than bread, but if we just stay at a bread level I think we can do something valuable here.

Let's bake some awesome bread and inspire others to join the taste movement.
