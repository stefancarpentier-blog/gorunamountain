We finally have a kick-off time for our oneyeartrip. So super excited about it all and I can't believe after all the waiting for it, it is now so close.

Mumu will leave for Mexico Dec 25th on a one-way ticket. BIG deal. Mumu quit her job, I have renters for our house, the dog is taken care off for the year, the chickens will have a safe and caring home and bees will have a new playground. We are ready to go!!!

Now the planning part of it all. Which countries at what time, which cities and areas to visit, which trips to do at each location..... Of course, not all will have to be figured out, but some structure will help up along the way.

Mexico will be 1st, the point of this is to calm down and be ready and tuned into what will come next. Some Beach, little activity and lots of quiet. WHERE?

South/Central America will be 1st after Mexico, ==> maybe 2-3 months. It is March by then and we can maybe string one country before we really want to be in Japan for Cherry Blossom and much much more. (Target April 1-15)

I would love to go to Iran, one of the cradles of humanity. I am getting VERY different suggestions on what to do. Travel group, solo, safe - unsafe, .... Some destinations seem to be clear there. Teheran, Isfahan, Yazd, ...

Japan and where to go???

After that, we might want to travel to Europe to check out the areas we might want to settle in. Portugal, Sardinia, Sicily, south of France, Basque area of France near the Pyrenees. Then go to Nuernberg, Germany, maybe some Scandinavian country? Maybe 3 months for that?

Then there is still south east asia to visit and India.

It is not at all defined yet, and as we go further out in time, it is clearly a total unknown.

I am looking forward to the process digging through all this.

For now, we are starting to say goodbye to people from here. One by one, heart to heart. At dinners or outdoor activities with friends.
