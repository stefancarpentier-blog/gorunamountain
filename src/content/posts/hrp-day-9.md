We had to leave this welcoming and nicely placed Refugio Belagua. The staff was simply disarmingly friendly, heartfelt and just great. We meet 3 people again, they slept near us last night but we all were to tired to talk then. So we caught up. Today is the last day with Christian. It was superb to have him and great fun, as always to hike with this man. I will truly miss you on the rest of the trip. None can replace you my friend. We meet a shepherd guard dog, the super gentle kind. He seems to let the sheep decide where to go, he just follows with them. We are also picking up Kim today, we meet already on the Gite. Happy Happy Times.
