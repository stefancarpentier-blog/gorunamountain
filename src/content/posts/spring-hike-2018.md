It is a real tradition by many standards now. Spring hike # 18 was underway last Saturday. Always the same route, always changing hikers, Flowers, and weather. The scenery is usually really amazing in the springtime with many changing Wildflowers along the way. This year we ha8 9 people to start with, one had to turn around at the Murietta falls intersection. It is always no fun to send back one of the team. But he was in good hiker company on the way back as he found a new Indian friend on the trail and they walked back together.

The Rest, 8 of us, went on and pulled it all through. Just over 9 hours from start to finish, Temperatures were truly ideal, almost a little warm on Rose Peak, but who would complain about that after such a long cold CA end of winter and Spring so far.

Ludger, Christian, Kim, Yan, Oscar, Ronja, Rosa and me.

The highlight of the hike where the 2 male Rattlesnakes battling it out on the side of the trail. Totally unimpressed by our presence.
