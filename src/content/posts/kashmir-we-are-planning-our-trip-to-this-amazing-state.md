Mumu and I have been invited by Junaid's parents to his wedding on September 1st. He is the person who made our last visit to India the incredible and memorable event it became. [Explore Culture & Tours (India) Pvt Ltd](https://www.facebook.com/exploreculturetours/) is a really really small operation, and each and every one working for them is a true and complete person. They are there to assist you in every way you can think of, know every step you make on your trip and they will make sure you get in touch with trustworthy drivers, guides, .... Accommodation is flawless to whatever your level you see needed. From basic to over the top luxury. Too many words from me and I will not be able to replace your trip with them. Long story short, we are invited to Kashmir, Srinagar for their wedding and we are humbled and amazed and happy. But we are also mindful of the recent developments in the area. The predominantly Muslim state has been a hotbed of conflicts between a number of groups and mostly the government. Tourists so far have been avoided, not completely though in the most recent past.
