We went to Croatia - Cavtat (pronounced Saftat) to celebrate Gilles' 80'th Birthday. He likes to travel to an all-inclusive place. A good way to travel worry-free.

Yugoslavia ==> Croatia??? I never really understood how the "disassembly" of Yugoslavia and the creation of 6 new countries happened, so here is my short version of what I learned. [LINK](https://www.infoplease.com/spot/timeline-former-yugoslavia) After WorldWar 2 the monarchy becomes a communist republic under Tito. His tight rein on Yugoslavia keeps ethnic tensions in check until his death in 1980. Without his influence, ethnic and nationalist differences begin to flare and eventually the 6 nations appear on the map with a nasty war in the middle. Croatia, Serbia, Slovenia, Montenegro (Black Mountain), Bosnia-Herzegovina, Macedonia appear on the map. Lots and lots of violence and death and all that comes with it of course.
