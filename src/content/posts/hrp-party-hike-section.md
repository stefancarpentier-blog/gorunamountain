After 2 zero hike days, away from l'Hospitalet and into Ax de Terme treated us well. Body and mind for some maintenance and relaxation, dearly needed. We picked up or two families from the train station and had a feast of togetherness and drinks, meals and conversations. I am so happy this all worked out. A little miracle. Day 38 We got up late, for jet lag and other needs. Fromage Blanc was the first course, pastries from the patisserie second course. And of we went to the Refugio Besine. A really short walk with 700m climbing on mostly easy footing. We were in the clouds all the way up, some form of wetness was always around us. At the hut, card games and more rain. Reasonable cold, so we went inside for the rest of the time till dinner. So good to be with the friends we have not seen for many years. Day 39 Starting from the hut with a group of 11 is not a quick task. We started on some rain, climbing through rocks and wet grass. I decided to stick with the slow team, chatting with, Mi-Suk and Kim. Things were easy till we reached the truly rocky parts. Visibility was near minimum 20-70m in the clouds. We made it to the hut with a few days off sun during lunch, then 4 boys decided to climb an extra credit pass for some views and it did pay off. Very rewarding, but the way down was tricky and not so much fun for me. The Refugio room was high up in the building and I ended up sleeping in the dining room on the table again because it was too sticky and warm fire me. Not a great night. Day 40 The day started like the last two.... In the clouds. The temperature is amazing for these foggy days, just the visibility is so limited. Very few sights worth sharing on days like this, but the hiking is so much easier when the temperature is reasonable. Yu managed to practically do the hike 2x with his 2h run up the two mountains. Incredible. We made it to the hut, the French host was friendly and motivated the food and beer excellent. I go to bed early today to catch up with the missed sleep yesterday. Sleeping outside of course. Day 41 Yu made it up the mountain one more time before breakfast. I slept like a log from 20.30 to 6.30. what a treat. Small breakfast and support late start of our two teams. I went with the turtle 🐢 team, the slow team of Thoma, Mi-Suk and Kim. What a treat of a walk it was the short day. Small forests, wide Meadows, grand views, good conversations, do in a gorgeous river. 10 of 10 today. And all that with dry feet. The Hill team came back s little tired of the steep down hill. Together we had a few beer and some food on the sunny and gently warm patio of a big restaurant. Sweet.
