I hear about [David Duke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Duke), a man who almost made it into the US senate, \[**CORRECTION**: was Governor of Luisiana\] made it into the house of Representatives in Luisiana and who made it his trademark to “**_look after the white_** _**race**_” and protect the world from inferior mindsets, preventing the mingling and mixing of humans of different origins and races.

I thought about this a lot and why I am so fundamentally opposed to it.

What makes me think this purist approach is so bad. Purist meaning, one group, sticking to itself, doing it's own thing can be better than groups mixing among each other. Who am I to judge someone's opinion and conviction without stomping on others' rights and beliefs? White supremacy is clearly proposing that the "white race" is better than the "black race". So I 1st thought I need to prove to myself that this is not true, find studies that show this fact. Well, I think it would still be a very weak argument in my view. It would still be a true argument, don't get me wrong. But I think it just compares one set of studies to another set of studies. ==> This will not get my point across big enough. I think the whole theory that ONE RACE will be better than a MIXED RACE is flawed. I am of course opposed to the fundamental idea that there is such a thing as supremacy of any race or any group of people.

I think I can prove that the mixing of all people is so much better than “sticking to our own kind”?

I want to go one level up. I want to step back and see which teams or groups in my professional life were successful and which ones were not. I am trying to augment the mix of the people of a team with respect to the quality of result they produce. Let’s make this simple by proposing a project.

**PROJECT**: We want to have a plan for the design and implementation of a pedestrian bridge over a busy street in the middle of a city.

Which team comes up with the best solution?

- Is a team with only structural engineers coming up with the best possible solution for this project?
    
- Is it a team with only structural engineers together with some leading experts in this area that have built these bridges over the last 30 years already?
    
- Is it a team with structural engineers together with members from the community, a kindergarten teacher, a few teenagers, members of the elderly community, a baker, a long-distance runner, an astronaut, a person in a wheelchair, a nanny and a member of the last bridge design that was a complete failure.
    

One would think that the team of expert engineers will come up with the best solutions. They know their subject matter, the group is small and agile and will come up with the solution fast, efficient and will get it right. They will be sure that their solution is the best and come out with confidence and come out with a great plan for that pedestrian bridge.

One would also initially think that the idea of bringing people into the team that have failed in previous projects would be a really stupid idea. After all, they messed up the last design, why would I give them a chance to mess up another one again. That is stupidity, no?

What good can a group of complete novices in this field of bridge design do to this team? This is overhead and will only bring this project to a grinding halt, slow it down unnecessarily. This is crazy talk to set up a team like this. We are designing a bridge, this is not a coffee and cake party.

Mixed gender team [LINK](https://www.trainingjournal.com/articles/opinion/why-mixed-gender-management-teams-perform-better)

Diverse team setup [LINK](https://bmchealthservres.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s12913-018-3812-4) [LINK](https://thenextweb.com/entrepreneur/2019/04/10/why-diverse-teams-make-better-business-decisions/)

To me as well as it is shown in these studies, it is a proven fact that the setup of the team is critically important to the ultimate success of the project. Also the mix of knowledge and experience, the wide variety of points of view makes the team stronger and better. The simplistic view of experts produce the best solutions is flawed. Why would I even want a _**pure**_ race, a _**pure**_ country, a _**pure**_ city. Why **pure** when you can have a mix?

In truth the purist and elitist approach is only about elevating yourself. It's about fulfilling one's need of power over others.

I have heard, thought and talked a lot about what goes on throughout the US, Europe as well as the world with the spread of the isolationist view of life. In politics, business and more. Isolating and elevating yourself from others seems to go on for more and more people in the last years.

It not only will lead us into some simplistic bubble, but also down a cliff of violence and even more wars and injustice. How do we change that.... I have no idea. I found it important to me to think about it and come to a thought through conclusion.

THINK and you can discuss. FOLLOW and you will only talk.
