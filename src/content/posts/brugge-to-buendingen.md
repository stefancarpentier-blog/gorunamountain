I have skipped posting for a few days, now here are some updates of my cycle trip.

I have made it through the flattest of flat-lands Belgium and rode through the Netherlands in under 30minutes. Now I am in Germany and make my way East, South-South-East. I am now in the world renown town of Buendingen..... (kidding). It is located just a little north of Frankfurt, Noth-East really. No more Flat tires since I exchanged the old tire. Go figure. Komoot is still treating me well with route finding.

I have made it through 50km of riding along a canal, more than I signed up for, more than I want to do again. It's flat and it is full of heavy industries. So far my most scenic day was getting out of Bonn and crossing through the Westerwald (east of the Eifel). Given the type of trails and roads I ride, it was a full day to be sure. 140km and 2000m climbing. I was happy to fall into bed after riding to Aldi to get anti-hunger supplies. It was the most scenic day on the tour so far.
