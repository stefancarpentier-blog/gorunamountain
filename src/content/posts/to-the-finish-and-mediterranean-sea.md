Day 46 A great morning in the campground with 8.00 breakfast with pastry and baguette. Then a car ride to the next town, where we managed to geta taxi cab to the next starting point, skipping a while day of the trail that we expect to be especially boring. We climbed a lot, starting only at 12.00 noon, in the heat, but found a lot of shade under truly magnificent trees. They kept changing and still stayed beautiful. We got to Illas with just enough time for a cold shower before dinner was served. Delicious dinner. The town had an unusual and weird touch.

Day 47 After a very weird and witchy might in Illas with dogs barking endlessly and chickens and roosters throughout the whole valley joining in a grand chorus several times throughout the night.... We all were ready to leave this place early. 7.10 departure. The day started already pretty warm but we could test a few times for blackberry break. The scenery is not that great, not bad either though. It took us 4 hours to get down to the valley and freeway Le Perthus. Checking on dinner availability at the next Gite revealed that they are fully booked. This info together with the temperature forecast of 34C tomorrow brought us to a long long group discussion on what to do. Go on our cutting the chords and calling it quit. We decided to move on with caution. Wait till 16.30 - 17.30 today to walk up the 800m to the finish point of the day, then wake up early and stay walking in the dark tomorrow morning to possibly escape the heat or at least be able to manage it. Let's see how all that will play out. Day 48 The final day finally arrived. We got started hiking in the dark at 6.00. A new experience for this trip. It was already warm at this time of the day, and I was a little shocked and fearful of what was to come. Oven, inferno, sauna.... Who knows. The wind was super super strong, that seemed good at the time. It will it be like a hair dryer in our faces inn the afternoon. Who knows how bad the temperature will be. We made good progress and found the one spring that is still active along the way. Impressive bites along the way and another set of views of the so long desired Mediterranean sea. We all were pretty focused on time and temperature do breaks were regular but pretty short. It got really hot and steep towards the end and shaded areas rare. But I'm about 8 hours we managed to complete this monster of a hike and arrive at the Sea, diving in about one minute after arrival. What a treat, what a day, what a journey and experience. We had a nice leftover meal along the beach together then struggled to find a restaurant still servings good at 21.00 and had a night in a hotel, trying to process what just happened
