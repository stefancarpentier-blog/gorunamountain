Can you believe it?! They came across the border again, this time they wanted more than Strassbourg and the Alsace Region, they wanted Nürnberg.

And Mumu and I even picked them all up at the airport. The weather did was not forecasted to be so great, but we had enough times in the sun to enjoy the old town and other sights around our little city. Gilles, Jeanine, Manu, Marine were the invaders and Marita, Mumu and I showed them around.

A short walking trip around town brought us to the oldest Bratwurst Restaurant in Nuernberg. "[Zum Guldenen Stern](https://goo.gl/maps/GAQkUb8cVzU1SRGU7)"  Built 1375, 1st mention as a tavern in 1419. It is far from a fancy place of fancy food. Typical Nuernberg small sausages with a variety of sauerkraut or Potato salad. Simple.

![BW](/photos/bw.png)

We went on to see the old town area in more detail and got all the way up to the Castel main area. Some rain set in and some Lebkuchen had to be had.

\

Nuernberg is pretty famous for its history in Toys, Kids Toys. My parents used to open their door and offer our bedroom for business people that wanted to attend the Toy manufacturer tradeshow in Nuernberg, but there were not enough hotel rooms available in town at the time. The adults got a little extra money from renting our room for 3 days, we kids got a few cool toys out of it for giving up our room. What a DEAL. Now we have a toy museum and we had to go in.

Coffee at my favorite place De Simo and the new favorite Cafe Constantinopel. Marita made us all a delicious meal for lunch and showed off her place, decorated with newly picked sunflowers from the nearby fields in Beringersdorf. Fabulous as always meal with laughter and the plenty obvious mention of the lack of bread (Baguette) on the table of a German meal we had a good time and set off for the Documentation center, a very noteworthy museum which does a great job showing what and how it all happened that Hitler came to power and what he all initiated, how others from the leadership competed against each other to impress the "Fuehrer" with even more and even harder treatment of the Jews. What always impresses me the most of this center is the way the exit is laid out. It is a stainless steel walkway that reminds the exiting visitor for the 1-2 minutes it takes to walk along that this sword goes right through the heart of one of the monumental buildings Hitler had constructed to show off the strength of the "Reich". It is now Impailed and to be considered dead for as long as we can make people remember these times and actions.
