We had a great night in this Gite' etape in Iraty. We could cook there and eat outside, meet some new people. A great time. Alec and Ryan cooked is some pasta we had a bottle of beer and wine, we could stay outside till late. Very good evening. The hike liked of with a super step party, right at the beginning, I felt strong and the views were far and fabulous again. But the wind was super strong. Because of this, we had to alter out route and decided not to climb the first 2000+m peak of our trip today. Lentil clouds on top convinced is. Right at the start of this traverse, I started feeling weaker, not bad, but maybe 75% of me. The stretch around these two peaks was harder work but again amazingly beautiful. After our lunch, strength came back a bit. Going down to our night camp Ardane. So so many sheep today, incredible. We seem to be all setup for a calm night today. Dog rolls in and out, it's warm and have our couscous coconut curry with veggies for dinner. Dates and other bars for dessert, tea to finish it all up. Another fabulous day.. It's now one week that we walk. 19km 700m climbing.
