We sent 2 days in Palermo. A city I expected to be chaotic and ridden with Mafia BS. None of this was true for us. Don't get me wrong, it did not become our favorite city all in one day, NO!! But Palermo presented itself to us quite pleasantly. We continued to have good food, coffee, and dessert, I got a good bike ride in, Mumu finally found her Puppets. And all that without being mugged or even feeling it would happen. Not bad, I would say.

We took the night Ferry to Naples, and there we were received by a city we found hard to like. The TAXI overcharged us and most of the streets we walked seemed somehow strange. I never felt as insecure anywhere in Italy as I felt here in only the 5 hours we spent there. The Pizza was good, and I really have no reason to go back there. But I do know I would go back with someone who knows this place really well.

On the train, we went and off to Florence in only 2.5 hours. We are now hunkering down in Poppi, in a house we rented for the 6 weeks we stay. We thought we will need to sign some house papers on Nov 12 (11/12 or was it 12/11) I just found out the hard way the signature will only happen on Dec 11. We will spend one extra month in Europe but we will go to London for a Family ThanksGiving then figure out what to do and where to go till we can sign this paper finally.

HOUSE: We finally have the impression we will get the house. We will get it late (May 2020) but we think we will. That is kind of a shock for us. Good shock, but still a shock. We found out we can get great tasting Mushrooms straight from our yeard.

\

I found a riding buddy for 3 weeks, Dave from Melbourne Australia. To kick things off we rode a quick trip to Talla and back. I think I should have been charged the Inter City Express fare. This man knows how to ride, and ride fast. I gave it all I had and we been on the road for 1.5 hours, did more than 40km and a bunch of climbing. My steely friend and I have not been on the move like this in a while. Dave could have left me in the dust still, but I was happy with the ride and with the fact I had a riding buddy.

Now, I am **WORKING** again. Yes, I am working. My 1st day today. I am helping out a very well known Blacksmith of the Valley as he has a crunch time and can use a hand. It was hard for me to stand in a shop for 8+ hours and doing physical labor as well as problem-solving with him. It was fun and will be fun tomorrow, but it was hard. Maybe I can get him to teach me a couple of skills when crunch time is over. I would love that, but let's see.
