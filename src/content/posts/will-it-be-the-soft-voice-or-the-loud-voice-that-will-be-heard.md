I think very few people actually KNOW what happened on this day, what IAM seriously interested in is who will be believed in this extraordinary public spectacle and hearing. How can we bring up and carry a person for this length of time who is this forceful and trying to be dominant at a time where the most intimate of experiences are shared with a far far to wide audience. Soft spoken and thoughtfully expressed words are in order to win my trust and believe. Dominating and loud words speak for a person that has a difficult access to his/her true heart and soul and maybe, just maybe had something to cover up with the tone and volume of choice.
