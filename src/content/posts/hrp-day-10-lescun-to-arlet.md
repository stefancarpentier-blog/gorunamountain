Today was the day off changing of the guards. Christian left us and he left big shoes to fill.... For Kim. He joins us for the next 8 days of the hike. The proper initiation procedure was applied. THE SLIDE. Kim passed and was accepted in the group, of course. We walked 5km on tiny roads, but but up hill steep. The scenery changed distinctly to higher mountains and rocky. Very out open and incredibly beautiful..... again. We climbed 1500m almost in one climb and it took a little bit of a tool on our legs. Even the traverse through this hugely open area was not only with simple footwork. I felt good today but was really tired in the end. A more basic Refugio waited for us, with sink washing but great people and a lake. Good was great and enough and we played two rounds of Mikado with a lot of fun. Tomorrow we want to move away from the main HEP trail and summit a mountain..... or not. Let's see. Stefan
