### English

Though this is my travel blog, I sometimes can't help it and have to vent in political stuff going on that gets to my core.

If it disappoints the one side and vindicates the other, I don't give Mueller any of the fault. I think it might be on **US**. We might carry outsized expectations of what a special council is able to price about a president and his staff. Here a great article about this.
