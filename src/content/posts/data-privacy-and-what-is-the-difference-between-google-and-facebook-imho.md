Mark just finished being grilled in DC, we all heard about it, listened to some of it. I was amazed by most of it. I am in shock and disbelieve how little control FB has over the data it collects and the data access it provides to it's clients (advertisers). Apparently FB till now really has no idea on who accesses which data from their store. FB in my view has yet to make a meaningful effort to be in complete control of all that. I see FBs main focus to be on making money. Privacy is at best an afterthought. Here is what I just received today from Google Analytics. I get a much warmer feeling of what is going on there. Feeling <==> reality. Who knows.
