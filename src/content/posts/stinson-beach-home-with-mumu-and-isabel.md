As it rained on Saturday, the day off or spring home we decided to replace it the day after with our OTHER favorite hike in the Bay. Mill valley to Stinson beach and back.

It's 7 miles one way and has 1300m of climbing. But lunch close to the beach and views to write about. We where tested with great weather as well.

The trail was partially under water and the crescen Creeks full of water. The beach looked like Mishr Niagra Falls and the hills had a great green to them. Good day!!
