London to Florence by plane with a little stop-over in Amsterdam Schipol and we are back in Tuscany. This part of the trip is very different on many levels. We know the area pretty well, we know good people here already, we want to find a place to live here. The house hunting will take up a lot of our time here. We are now one week into it and it is hard work. HARD WORK.

\

But we also were invited to Anita's Communion celebration. A big party with many people and lots of food. Delicious food. I contributed my part with 16 herb Baguette and 4 sourdough loaves with hazelnut and walnut. What a heartfelt event, what a set of amazing people, what a treat to be part of this. Thank you.

\

We spent two days going to Lucca and to the coast. The BnB we managed to get into was a historic GEM, the Beach and water seemed unreal as the color was so blue, it seemed radioactive.
