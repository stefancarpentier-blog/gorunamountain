Reinhold, a University friend of mine managed to get my other University Cycle friend Robert to commit to a ride after their work hours. What a treat. We did a number of rides together many years ago and getting these busy minds onto a bike at the same time, on the same road if a feat. Thank you!! Robert and I met 17.15 at his work and started riding for 45 min to pick up Reinhold alone the way. We went through well-known roads and areas, even for me, at the beginning. The pace picked up at times and I wondered where the promised BeerGarden would be. I brought no water, no food because of it. 1st warning came from me. “STEFAN HUNGRY” ————— no response. 20-30 min later my second warning came across after we passed through Schnaitach and kept heading north, further out. “Stefan’s blood sugar is dropping”. Finally, Reinhold had mercy with my poor body, not my soul. 2 little bars and a bottle of water. The worst was averted and i could continue to ride after two handfuls of fresh cherries from the tree. We went through Hilpoldstein, a little village with a castle which I almost forgot how amazingly beautiful it is from one side. Memories. We went for a quick ice cream stop along the quicker Bundestrasse way back home but back on our bikes and saying goodbye to Reinhold who also had about 30km to ride till back home. Robert was nice enough to offer a cold glass of beer for me along the way home for me. What a treat it was to ride along memory lane and spend quality riding times together. I got home just before dark and we clocked about 80km together. Not bad for an after-work little ride.

All the riding was part of my prep for the tour that is just about to start. Wednesday if the start for myriad to Poppi and then maybe all the way to Apulia Lecce.

I just started this ride yesterday and I might have asked for good weather a little too desperately, a little too hard. The first two days were really hot with up to 40C. Riding early was the key. But I had a very good reason to start at 9.00 only on the 1st day. My good friend Kurt decided to join me on my 1st-day ride, then ride back home the same day, in this heat. We truly had a great ride along the old Donau-Main canal from Nuernberg (the north end) to Beilngries (almost the south end). It almost doubled our trip distance, but talk about a worthy environment and finally a full 8 hours to talk to my friend. I loved it. THANK YOU for coming along for the kickoff of my ride. I loved it. Kurt must have clocked 170-180km that day, in 38-40C heat. That was tough I am sure.

A few lesions of learning came up for me, thanks to Kurt. We passed by the Brueck-kanal which has a bridge that was built to allow the kanal to get over the Schwarzach (river). Built from 1836-1846. [DETAILS.](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwig-Donau-Main-Kanal#Br%C3%BCckkan%C3%A4le)

I was welcome by my friend Oliver and his wife that night. With all the treats I could not have expected. BBQ and homegrown cucumber salad and much much more for my super hungry belly. Good talks, but I had to keep them kinda brief as I was really tired after the hot and long ride. ~130km

![DSC01204-s](/photos/dsc01204-s.jpg)

Today I am riding alone and trying to reach closer to the Alps, the second part of the ride is along the Lech river, which assured the hot part of the ride to be under trees and mostly flat. Which is Oberbayern, for the locally inclined and the language is easy to recognize, food and styles are different. These are starting to be TRUE Bavarians, not like us people from Nuernberg who really are true Franconians. Much better people we are of course ;-). Never the less, it was another long and hot day and happy I was to get to the hotel. 162km
