As I rode a little faster than expected, I now had 10 days in my birth town.

Lots of food to eat, places to visit and people to meet. And on top of it all it seems like I am getting the treat of warm warm weather and no rain. What a treat. I went hiking with Mom, and a little rock scrambling too. Good job Mom!!! Meeting my old school time friend of 40+ years after he built his new house far far away from big cities, just the way they both (Nadine and him) like it. Good time, lots to catch up so we did not end up riding bikes at all.
