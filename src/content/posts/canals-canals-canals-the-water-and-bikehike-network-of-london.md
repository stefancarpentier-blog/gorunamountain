I have now been in London a few times and learned to really appreciate the comfort the old canals provide to Londoners. In the old days, they were used for hauling heavy loads into town by animal-drawn cargo ships. They are now used for rejuvenation. Boat tours are scenic and biking and hiking along these is a really enjoyable and fast way to get to places in London. A little over an hour ride brings you into town from Heathrow airport. All but the first few km are along canals. That beats the street craziness any time. There are plenty of pubs, coffee shops, and hip pizza shops to squelch your every need.

The last week's I must have ridden more than 100km on these.
