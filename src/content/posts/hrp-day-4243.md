Day 42I had a horrible night with snoring companions and a beating heart because of the 14.30 espresso I drank. Moved out to the Picknick Bench to finally get some sleep, only to be awaken by rain. Inside I slept in the entry room on the floor, with motion controlled bright lights. Not optional. I need another good night again. We had to say a sad good bye to the 8 friends of this last 4 days of hiking. It was amazing to have you part of the trip, incredible that all the details happened as we all wanted, super that none of the possible bad travel events happened and we could see you again. Some of you after 4+ years. Incredible. With our new team member Erica, friend of Rosa, we got started and managed to see a bunch of beautiful lakes in the way down to Bolquere ski resort town. We caught a good store open still and got some food for the night and the next day. We managed to get a hitch to Enye with the first or second car we put the thumb out. That is a record. A gentle climb through beautiful forrest on an easy path. This way we will shave off distance and climbing of the day tomorrow. Found a great camping spot right next to the river. Awesome. Day 43 It was a cold night but nothing serious. Early start and lots lots of wind with cold temperatures almost the whole day. But the day was one of my favorite days of the trip so far. The climbs were plentiful but easy, the views amazing and breathtaking. We probably summited 6-7 peaks in the process, one close to 3000m. Arriving at a Spanish Refugio is always a treat. So was today. Yes, of course, how can I help you and a smile is a great standard to have.
