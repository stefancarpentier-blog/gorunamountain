**English**

**I**t is finally coming together. In Yelapa we ended up in an amazing place called **Vereda**, a little and gorgeous place just off the walking path. The small green gate is such an understatement to what we found behind it. A very tastefully placed and skillfully built BnB. A pool in the middle of it all. Art is found in a lot of areas here. The kitchen is so well laid out and done, Jeff, the owner, gets me into real trouble as I already know I will need to build something like this ….. sometime soon. Mumu will make sure of this.

**Y**elapa seems like a pretty small and tightly knit community. The people living there seemed to be genuine and kind, have no real need to worry about any kind of schedule at all. We got to meet some real characters from here.

- **Bill Livingston**: the movie producer, videographer for HUGE places like National Geographic, PBS and more. An overall adventurous and super nice guy who has visited and taken in so many places in this world. LINK
- **Banana John**: Hard to describe and put my finger on him. Artist in constant transition, musician, life juggler?! [LINK](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cFw9ZVI6QIM) to video
- **Ron** the musician
- **Jeff** the owner of the [Vereda](https://g.co/kgs/wPAA5Y) BnB

![](/photos/bill-mumu.png)

People seem to get a handshake or hug style greeting only after going to a restaurant more than 3 times. So welcoming, it was great. We were able to get really great food, not the Mexican food we expected, but delicious and fresh vegetables put together to create a well rounded and interesting meal. Can you say fresh salad, Rice noodles, Octopus, blue corn - hand pressed tortillas

**Mexico City**: We are now on our way to Mexico City. A total change of pace and environment. 2,250m in elevation, the biggest city in North America where we expect to find culture and tradition in abundance. We are staying in the Condesa district. Supposedly a real hip area. But this Saturday afternoon, I have to say, we were not yet fully impressed with it. The drive into town was really smooth, no traffic, not too much noise, sidewalks are pretty empty..... Let's see what tomorrow brings.

Die größte Stadt Nordamerikas, in der Kultur und Tradition in Hülle und Fülle zu finden sind, ist 2.250 m. Wir wohnen im Stadtteil Condesa. Angeblich ein echter Hüftbereich. **Mexico**: Nous sommes maintenant en route pour Mexico. Un changement total de rythme et d'environnement. 2 250 m d’altitude, la plus grande ville d’Amérique du Nord où nous espérons retrouver culture et traditions en abondance. Nous restons dans le quartier de Condesa. Soi-disant un vrai quartier de la hanche. Mais ce samedi après-midi, je dois dire que nous n’avons pas encore été complètement impressionnés. **Città del Messico**: siamo diretti a Città del Messico. Un totale cambiamento di ritmo e ambiente. 2.250 metri di altezza, la più grande città del Nord America, dove ci aspettiamo di trovare cultura e tradizione in abbondanza. Siamo nel quartiere di Condesa. Presumibilmente una vera area alla moda. Ma questo sabato pomeriggio, devo dire, non ne siamo rimasti completamente colpiti.
