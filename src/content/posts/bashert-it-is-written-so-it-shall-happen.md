**English**

So Muriel and I finally met in Puerto Vallarta (PV). Yepeee. We found the place we booked easily but found it was not pleasing at all to what we expected.

Bashert == It is written - so it shall happen.

Trying to find a nicer place to stay for the week was the next order of business of course. The most important factor of it all was for me to wear the "**Support local Bees**" T-shirt. The key to it all. We ended up meeting [Bill Livingston](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm4888997/) , a filmmaker for National Geographic on the paths in Yelapa. The Bees were the kickoff of our conversations.

**It is written, so it shall happen.**

Before we knew it, we sat in Bill's little Cabana and drank excellent Tequila accompanied by great stories and insights about the town. We got in touch with Jeff who built and runs a place called Vereda in Yelapa. A beautiful place with all and more we needed. Time and space to rest and relax, giving us the time to realize what is about to start; we are in the process of starting.
