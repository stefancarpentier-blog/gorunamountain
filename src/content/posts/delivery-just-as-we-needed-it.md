### English

Mumu and I were in need, deep need for some slow down, comfort, nice people, some exercise and a whole lot of quiet. Thank you Sucre, you delivered as needed.

Takakumba Hostel offered us the chance to cook and prepare it own meals, bake some bread and meet some people. So so good.

So there is really not to much to write about this as not much happened other that both our minds are more grounded than 7 days ago.

The only unpleasant part was the ride midnight Awakening from the immigration police, checking out passports. I checked up with the hostel owner and it happens rarely but it's not totally uncommon. I would like to write to the boss of police or immigration about this and what this does to innocent travelers and their sleep patterns....

We are now on our way to lake Titicaca, the highest navigable lake on our planet. A few days on Isla del Sol with some hikes should do us well. Let's see what the next few days bring to us.
