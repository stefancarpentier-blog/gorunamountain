Great great two days in the backcountry with just amazing weather and really stable snow. Stay we started from echo Lodge going along the story gentle ridge towards Becker Peak and Talking mountain [LINK](https://caltopo.com/map.html#ll=38.83398,-120.05741&z=15&b=mbt&a=sf), then heading down to echo lakes. 2nd lap was 80℅ back up to the ridge, the runs were so smooth, amazing fun. Back over the lake, it ended up being pretty cold. A quick climb up to the road and smooth gliding asking the road back to the car. 2 ND day we got a late 11 am start and headed to Carson pass parking lot. Several groups were active there. We headed east towards Red Lake area to get three more amazingly fun descents 6km 650m elevation. The snow started off to be little sticky for skinning, but it cooled down ascents as well as descents worked out to be great. Back along the road to get back up to the pass. Great day(s).
