CAPS_Blog


After the /HRP 2024/ , I kinda fell in love with the Pyrenees, more than already and over the next year decided another significant time, maybe another crossing through this beautiful mountain range was in order for me. Contemplating ideas and possible scenarios, I ended up with CAPS.   Coast to Coast Across the Pyrenees, Southern route. This time by bike.  As I could not find any premade routes that fill my bill of so many aspects (length, amount of climbing, beauty of the route, accommodation, northern vs. southern Pyrenees….. I had to come up with one myself. Not as easy as it sounds, plenty opportunities of small and big mistakes. In the end I think I managed to avoid many, still made a few of them. Finding the right team of capable as well as a little foolish was another, finding the time and managing all the rest of the details……

DAY 2


AUG 16, 2026
― SAINT JEAN PIED de PORT 2 Iraty 2 Larrau
After a noisy and eventful night were one of our bikes was almost stolen by on of the drink party tigers we got off to an extremely slow start. We all had to learn a lot how to hard s set of 7 independent and different cats. I think we might have left camp at 10.30. Another day with more than 1300m climbing was ahead of us. More Basque food and great views kept us pedaling. We had amazing decedents and a herd of sheep blocking it poorly for s while. So cute. The little campground had a little Imbiss Stand with great pizza and we got to talk to a Spanish Anthropolog. What a treat..

DAY 3


AUG 17, 2026
― Larrau 2 

The next morning started with we tents again and no food fire breakfast at the camp and the local bakery was not open that day….. right out of camp we stayed climbing super step again. Just like the last day. I started questioning my choice of hearing for this tour. I NEED LOWER GEARS for this trip. Patience and some experience and a bucket of determination got us through the clouds and on top of the next pass, crossing into Spain. We also crossed our track of the HRP a second time that day. The views, with the clouds and mountains poking through where beautiful. A camp 3km out of town was the place for the night. Big camp ground but welcoming people and reasonable food.

DAY 4


AUG 18, 2026
― 2 Yaca Detour around the forest fire
The morning started with an excellent year from a friendly basket lady, serving pain o chocolate, croissants and some cake. Truly excellent. But the 7 of us almost cleaned hey out and several off the local clients in line walked away without their breakfast treat…. Happy but still sad. We continued a little bit down the beautiful valley and turned off to an even less populated street. Finally a pass that was not above 10% . My legs applauded. The way down we managed to lose our downhill rocketeer Tom as he raced through a crucial intersection. For him that meant to climb back up and meet us in …….. in the region on Aragon. We found a truly amazing deli which fuled is with pizza like flat bread and delicious sweets. Following this, with really heating up conditions, we ran a speed train down the valley towards Yaca. Managed to squeeze in a river dip, and Kim somehow was handed a bottle of water and energy drink by a complete stranger, nice and cold. People are amazing. The “last little stretch" to jaca proved more adventurous. The only real street to get us there was closed for for danger, the Autobahn, out of question we made our way slowly through remote houses and gravel roads to Jaca. The campground told is initially, no no no FULL. NO MORE.  A few minutes and many words later she guided is to a small area. Not pretty, but doable. The next visitors were plain out rejected. So lucky for us. We chatted up a ditch gut on motorcycle and one from Catalonia. Warm good people. Dinner and then a thunderstorm and rain that warranted putting up the tent again for sure. What a light show.

DAY 14


AUG 28, 2026
― 2 Perpignan 

Leaving this welcoming, warm and beautiful place was made only harder because of the cute and gorgeous Husky they had on the property.
![Local Image](./husky.jpg)
We got up early and started breakfast already at 7.00. still out from us till 8.20 to get rolling and started. We opted for the adventurous route done hill, avoiding a 200+m climb up to the highway. It was a fun ride/home down a really really step hill, we learned what these bikes can and cannot do. Ending up at a thermal bath we refilled with water and Rosa and Fabian took off to get their bike box for the plane. We took it a little easier and had a bike design store break and found really nice local streets to avoid the main highway which was not too pretty and reasonably busy. Then also Kim and I broke of from July and Tom and found a nice small parallel street almost all the way into Perpignan. Not hard. not hot. not pretty. We meet up with Rosa and Fabian, and headed for the ultimate goal. The Mediterranean beach. Another 15km and a few wrong turns and we where the. Down with the t shirt, into the water we go.
