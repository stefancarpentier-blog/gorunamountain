We decided to check out some unlikely places of the bay. Port Costa was the choice. A small and old Town of some 190 people left living there. 1879 to 1930 or was a growth town. Shipping was a big deal. But the railroad and the Carquinez bridge put an end to it's glory. All that is left is a hotel, bar and really good restaurant. But don't forget the charm it brings.
