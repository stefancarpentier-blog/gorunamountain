Mumu, Isabel, Francois, Michela and me went to experience Laurie Anderson yesterday. It was the most unique and really impressive audio experience. It did not really have a start our end time, no assigned seats, you walk through the place and where ever you are, you create your unique experience of it. A walking sax player, looped violin player, six guitars in a circle feeding back to each other a modified saxophone making supper low sounds and then Laurie with her small violin type thing and a bunch of high tech hardware. Well done.
