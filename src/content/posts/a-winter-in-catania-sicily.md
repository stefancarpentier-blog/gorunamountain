But NO, nO, No, no, it did not grow on me. Sicily So, during the summer Muriel and I decided what to do about this trouble. After bouncing around some ideas, looking at a lot of data, stories, and pictures. After looking at endless accommodation sites and making choices, we ended up in Catania Sicily. Downtown Catania, 20min by foot to the beach, coffee shop in front of the house, Etna visible almost all day kind of Catania. We wanted the stimulation of a city, a variety of different foods, warmer temperatures, and proximity to the sea. CHECK. We are here now for 1 week and I like it. I have been in the sea 2x already, walking for hours on the beach 2x, riding a bike 3x, riding a unicycle 2x, found fresh, local oranges for juice, have eaten well so far and have eaten international (cucina non tipici).
