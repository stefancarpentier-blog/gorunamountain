## I think sharing is scary.

I am NOT talking about sharing your last drop of water with your friend of course. I am referring to our omnipresent SocialMedia platforms and how we all use them. Sharing an interesting article with my friend, cool. Getting the almost identical messages flashed back on my screens, 100 times….. Not COOL.

[![](/photos/sharing-is-scary.png)](nonprofitcenters.org)

We share things we like things on SocialMedia. Repetitive messages and articles are offered over and over again. What we share, what we like, what we search for gives these algorithms an idea of what excites us. Our excitement is good for these corporations.

As a result, we read and hear less dissenting information ==> But we also become less tolerant of other views, other opinions. We react faster and faster to small bits of information, and don't check the validity of this information. 

SocialMedia providers make more money the more we share, also the more time we spend with them.

I know I am changing because of all this. I am assuming others are too. 

I know the way I interact with each other is changing. It's changing fast and it's changing not in a direction of _listening_, _inclusiveness_ and _trying to understand_ each other, not in a direction of _togetherness_, but towards "_island-building_", _isolation,_ and _pushing_ each other away. I quickly disregard someone as a person, just because of ONE bit of information I heard/saw about them. It is exactly the opposite of what we as a world society need to solve the existential problems we need to fix. We want to be able to survive in the long term on this planet.

* * *

**Here is my question then**: 

- We know we are going in a direction that is hurting us 

- We know some of the main players that dictate the way we receive information

- We know the main players make Billions and Billions of $$$ in the process

- Why are we not doing anything about the actual cause of this problem?

- Why do we seem to fiddle around the edges?

- Why do we not really change the situation we are in, the direction we are heading? 

* * *

We have options to FORCE these algorithms to change. I see this as one way to start addressing this. But we would be forcing big players to agree to make a whole lot less money or invite them to make their money differently, doing less damage to the people and societies using their services.

No call to action here. No Newsletter to sign up for. No group to join. 

## BUT

Do what is in your reach, what is in your power to make changes in your reach, what is in your power to make changes to.
