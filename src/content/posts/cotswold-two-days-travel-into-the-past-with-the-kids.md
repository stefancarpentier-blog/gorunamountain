It truly seemed like being thrown back into the past when we drove out of London, North, and getting to the Cotswolds area, north of London. Thank you, Paolo, for your great recommendations of little towns and even littler towns to visit. Coming from Osaka Japan, I truly felt like stepping out of a Time machine, 100 years back at least.

An abundance of green and old houses, built in the local traditions was a little overwhelming for me but cool and enjoyable. Some areas seem to have been taken over by the rich layer in British society, other Families seem to have stayed local and stayed the course of tradition.

Thanks for taking the time up there with us. Awesome.
