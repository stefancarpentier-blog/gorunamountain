I finally need to write about this. It has been highly educational, mind-opening, saddening, refreshing, and many many more adjectives could follow here. As a society, we have been going through a long and I think pretty steep learning curve about sexuality, sexual preferences, sexual identity, transitioning processes, identification processes, and migration processes between two of these states or descriptors. I do not claim completeness, I don't claim to be on top of it all. To be sure, I had to read up on and talk to people about many of the different types and processes that people go through, want to go through or have to go through to change their identity. It is highly complex and it is highly confusing to me. Many many emotions came up in me while learning about this process. Like in many learning processes, I went through denial, pushing it away, minimizing it, NIMBY it, and acceptance of it. I think I reached the state where I know enough about it and I started analyzing all this and what we, as a society all do with this complex and confusing data, not to talk about any person in detail yet at all. All this is still fully conceptual, not personal. I am trying to understand what it is I am looking at, who is involved, and what impact it all has and can have.

## The discovery and the choice

1st, I have to say that I am always overcome with the deepest sadness when I hear about people going through any kind of sex change process or going through a process of redefining themself. Why am I sad you ask? I am sad to see any human suffer or be hurt. And however this one person gets to the decision to not be able to identify with their own sex, sexuality or body, it seems like they are in for a long long time of really hard psychological, mental, and sometimes also physical pain. That is why I feel sad at that time. I am also very concerned about the seemingly increasing number of people going through this type of re-definition process. We are exposing people at a really young age to information that is highly complex and it influences them. What I am concerned about is that we expose teenagers to this information, at a time that is probably one of the hardest times in their lives. I know the teenage years were hard for me, and believe me, I had it easy for many reasons. I am a white male, born in Germany, to German parents, in a household that was not rich, but rich in love and care. We are exposing what seems like all of our teenagers to many more questions they need to answer for themself during this period. Don't get me wrong, choices are good. But many choices make it harder. Many more choices make it a lot harder, now and for a long period of their life. Choice is good, don't get me wrong. The damages that people endured and still endure while NOT coming out and making the changes needed are heart-wrenching..... But, please, make no mistake, choice has its cost too. To the person and to the society. ==> The decision process for this individual is much more complex and as a result, it must be more error-prone. The person might make a big decision in their life, only to find out 1-2-13 years later, the decision was rushed, made too early in life, that this solution might not have been the solution that was truly needed and was best at the time. There is a huge cost to making suboptimal decisions of this magnitude. For that person and for all of us as a society.

## Rights and duties of the individual; We all are the same, I respect you and you respect me

Let's say I decide to go through this process of a sex change. Who am I doing this for and why am I doing this? It needs to be done for ME and my personal needs, however intricate they may be. If I feel like I need this, I need to go through this process, with all the pains and gains it delivers. The rest of you people, I expect you to accept my decision and treat me with respect. I DON'T do this for any reason other than MY reasons. I also cannot expect the whole world to change, just because of me and the process I am going through. That would be crazy. I also need to try and make the life of everyone around me the least impacted by this, because it is ME who needs this change, no one else asked me to do this. If I feel very strongly that I need to go to the bathroom that best suits me, I behave like a good human and expect everyone else to behave like good humans too. If I talk to someone, they will address me with the name I tell them I have, with respect and decency. It is a give and a take, it is a process that needs tolerance from BOTH sides. It is a give and a take. I would cause a problem, in my opinion, if I started demanding the whole rest of society to treat me differently than everybody else. I want my own bathroom, I want to be addressed with "THEM" "IT" "THEY" ...... In my view it is an act of arrogance, demanding special treatment because of something I felt like I needed to do. How should everybody else know how to address me, by just looking at me? Even if this situation is playing in a company setting and I am known to my co-workers. I was "HE" and now I am "HER". OK, If I look like a woman, I will make it easy for my co-workers to remember and address me as an "HER". What if the week after I come up with a new rule and want to be addressed with "YOURS TRULY". Or to top it off, I am putting myself in the non-binary category and demanding pronoun changes on a daily basis? ==> I see this as arrogant, intolerant, and problematic. This is why I say, You are You, and I am Me. I will treat you with respect and demand the same from you. I will not try to make myself special and unique by posing these demands. I know or can somewhat imagine life is a truly hard thing to master, adding any sex re-defining process on top of this will only make it so much harder. But I am very sorry to say, if anyone decides to go through this process it is his/her life and his/her choice and he/she needs to deal with most of this, not downloading it to the rest of society. I am willing to help, but it must be a sharing effort, and you must carry your hard and heavy share. I am just a bystander, trying to work through my own issues and own problems.

## Attention is a limited good - we need to focus on existential problems

I think I made clear that the topic is dear to me and my heart, I don't want to hurt anyone or accuse anyone of any wrongdoing in any specific instance. I am trying to look at this topic from a little bit above the ground. I am trying to bring some abstractions into this discussion. I find it the only way to stay on any course. When I listen to the news, I used to hear a lot about topics that touch on sexual orientation (before the Ukraine war that is). When I look at politics, I see a large amount of attention to be given to this very topic as well. I just heard a report about the Berlinale Film Festival and its nominations and winners of certain prizes. All these areas are occupied with working through this topic, they are in my view clogged with this topic. It seems like the only way to be nominated for a prize at the Berlinale, the only way to get news coverage is to report about a sexual orientation-related topic. And I can tell you, I am tired of it. These cases are hard and important for us as a society, but they are not all topics we need to work on. They cannot be all we report on or legislate on all we see movies about. In my opinion, we do have a number of global problems that involve the very existence of our planet with us humans. One more reason for adjusting how we treat this topic is fairness. We are not treating ~50% of our world population equal to the other 50% today (male <--> female). That is a problem that needs a solution for many years and we are only inching toward equality (woman's rights). I don't know how many people belong to the LGBTQ+ group (I read a number of about 1% of our population). What I do know it is less than 50% of society. It seems like the news coverage, and political engagement is strongly tilted toward the LGBTQ+ topic, weighted toward the number of people on this planet. Let's work on topics that are the mosrt urgent and the most relevant, not on the ones that people vocalize the best about.

I feel like we need to address the problems and issues around LGBTQ+ topics. But I also think we HAVE to save the planet we are living on, if we lose this, we have nothing to live on anymore and no LGBTQ+ topic matters.

## Learning

This is what I think about this topic right now. It is incomplete and based on what I read, heard, and experienced in my life so far. I will learn more and just my opinion along with that. If you feel differently, have input, or want to speak out, please tell me. I want to learn more.

**_After discussions with some nice and smart people... I want to add something._**

I think what I am really all hung up about is not the people that go through this process, but I am fed up with the Movement of LGBTQ+. It seems to me to be not very well defined (look at the [WiKi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LGBT) page). It seems to me to be unstructured to the core, inclusive to almost everyone, trying to do everything and really IMO getting nothing really done. The main thing IMO is time. This type of change needs time in society. And it seems to me, this is not what is really given. This is what is bugging me so much.
