**August 21**

I started in Karlstadt before 9am. The BnB Zum Fehmelbauer was a real treat. The room was truly tiny but all I needed, the people sure nice and the dinner as well as breakfast. Exceptional.

The route of the day started with a pretty steep but short climb on a gravel road, away from the Landstrasse. Awesome.  The morning was kinda slow going with more small country roads and lots of little towns. All is good there as I really like these stretches. It gives more time to see and smell and hear. On my little pre-lunch stop in Schweinfurt area, I went up to the Marktplatz and got spice of cake where I managed to see an old man walking in with his little Rauhardackel. It was so cute to see their little interactions. LOVED IT.

Riding to lunch was fine. The lunch spot was a pretty traditional Frankonian Gasthaus. Well equipped with the drunk crew. What a brain-dead bunch, I feel so sad to see that.

Getting into Bamberg was easy, finding a hotel, not so much but I got a Great basic little place “Hotel Lieb”. A German Guy with his Thai wife. Both seem REAL and good people. I like this a lot.

Going to Bamberg to find out some about the city as well as try the two smokey beers the town is known for. Mom known one is Schlenkala.
