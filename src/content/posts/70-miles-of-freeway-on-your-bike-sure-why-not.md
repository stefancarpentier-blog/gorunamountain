### English

After the amazing stretch to and through Big Sur we knew the scenery would change.... A lot. What I did not see coming was the number of cars passing iys at 65mph, at a distance that was way under my comfort zone and the constant noise level I would have around me. For the next three days we rode ~230 miles and from that at least 70 miles under these conditions. I was not prepared for that and did not like one minute of it. Call me a whiner or what ever. This is not my type of riding. Adding 5 flat tires in 24 hours and me the German softy was done with. As we rolled into Santa Monica at 5.30pm in the dark, the beautiful sites we have seen could no longer outweigh the mental stress I felt under. I had to tell Chris I was done with riding this tour.

I do not want to overshadow the amazing accommodation and hospitality we got through Warmshower.org . What a super treat we received during our stay in Santa Barbara. An amazing place and view, great conversations and super people. Thank you so much.
