We have this tradition to bring good California weather to London, every time we go there. So, what will it be this time?

While Japan was a truly great and fulfilled experience, we were very glad to get to spend some Family time with Cassandre and Gabriele. Doing that under the sun would be so much more fun, so we brought some along. Leaving the futuristic Osaka was not that hard for me.

ASIANA flight was flawless and we arrived tired in their apartment. 5 minutes after great hugs and shower, both Mumu and I went straight to bed. We were SO tired. I got to play with the sourdough starter that Cassandre maintained for a year and kicked off some country loaves. I don't think the 1st one hit the kitchen table for more than one hour till it was gone. I think it must have been really bad bread. I picked up the Brompton from the store and started my mental recovery routine. Riding! Was that a treat to finally ride again and ride a bike that actually fits my body. Mumu and I were able to cook and prepare breakfast for the whole team and spoil the kids a little. How fun is that!? Eating a salad, vegetables and things we can actually pronounce the name of food we eat for a change. Being able to have a conversation with anyone, anywhere. Simple joys of being in England.

 

\

the Second one [LINK](https://www.strava.com/activities/2397151585) was more remote and I needed a train to get to _**Reading**_ and then rode through amazing countryside and incredibly green hills and fields for a few hours. What a mental treat that was.

\

Allison and Nigel invited us to meet in Winchester for a California reunion and tour through the old town, then a small ride with Nigel to an amazing lunch place in the area. What a treat to ride through this area. It felt 100% British riding. I loved to exchange with the two. who moved back to the UK about 2 years ago. Great times.

More cooking for yourself and the "kids" which was a treat as well. I miss it a lot in these times of travel. I managed to do some bread too. So good. But more about that in the next post.  London rocks though, with it's clear opposition to "T".
