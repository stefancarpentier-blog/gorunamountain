Mumu and I finally made it to my home town Nuernberg. Being able to see my Mom for a while was way way overdue and the rest of the family crowd in similar ways.

My Mom's place is always stylish, beyond clean and she is as welcoming as you can possibly want it. ==> Son and Mumu are very well taken care off. In all and anyways. 1st and 2nd there is always food. You all need to understand one concept my Grandmom seems to have implanted in all family members. If you have guests and ask if they want food, more food or anything in this arena ==> you don't really want to give. You need to buy it, prepare it and load it on their plate, no question asked. Only if they violently refuse and make their point several times that no more food fits in, then. And only then are you allowed to back off and stop cooking. I am overdoing this of course, but you get the idea.

\

### Reunion with my Rock-Lobster

After almost one full year of separation from my bicycle, I found her again and I found her in good condition, no hard feelings and a great reunion. Just a little air and a few drops of oil on the chain. She was good for a spin in the Pegniz valley and then we two went "all the way". The Frankenjura is just north of Nuernberg and holds a wealth of streets and trails that all are calling me, all the time. "RIDE ME" come and ride me, I missed you for 25 years. It took some time to become familiar with the area again, but a few times I could confidently disagree with Google Maps and found known territory. Getting lost when I was wrong seemed also part of the game of getting back together.

\

### The Alps with the extended Nerb family and more new/old friends

Christian and Michael seem to have gone up to the Buchsteinhuette for as long as they can remember. Now they belong to the club that owns these two huts. One is run by a "Wirt" and the other is there for the club members and their guests. Just bring your food and you are good for a weekend. Or don't and eat at the "Wirt" and worry about even less. It's a very comfortable and sizable hut, giving room for about 40 people. Electricity is provided by their own water power turbine, backup is a diesel generator. The heat comes from local wood, fun to be supplied by yourself. This is not your Sierra type setup, my friends. Alms on almost every path you might desire to hike, one after every 45min or so. This is incredible. They provide you food at 1600m elevation and it tastes good, does not cost the world and comes with an amazing view, a friendly smile and advise for anyone who feels the need for it. We bought some local honey, were a little early for local cheese but managed to get raw milk and Buttermilk. And it tasted, ... you guessed it. It tasted like cow and actual milk. Amazing. Michael did an amazing job setting up the place, getting us all organized, setting up the Bondfire, BBQ, and and and so much more. This man is on fire. Thanks again, Michael. TO say meeting Chisstian again is an understatement. Thanks for inviting us to this yearly ritual of your team.
