A great article about what influence the Brown family had a lot of say. No other family has wielded as much influence over California as the Browns: Pat Brown served as governor over two terms beginning in 1959, while his son Jerry is now in his fourth term and second round as the top state official. But the family’s intertwining with California history did not begin there.
