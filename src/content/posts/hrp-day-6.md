So far, the best weather of the trip. 10-20C some clouds, and almost no wind. The climbs were steep, the way I like it, undiscovered and empty and amazingly beautiful. The horses fashion seems to shift towards blonde with brown for in this area, and they are beautiful. Had amazingly good lunch along the way at Chalet Pedro, incredible. Them another step road climb into Iraty. Great day, great time. I smile a lot these days. Stefan
