We left Gavarine really late, but with one more team member. Gilad, an Israeli young man we meet briefly and had great discussions with over dinner decided to join us for this segment 3. After I strongly encouraged him to lighten his pack by a few kilograms, he switched over with us to the HRP route. Great from the military, he is for enough to do all we can do and then some. Climbing of it of the Gavarine valley, it was still cold, but great for climbing. I got a new set of hiking poles, aluminium ones and they are much much heavier than my previous ones. My shoulders complained about it the whole day. Nowadays, 1500m climbs on the morning don't seem to be a big deal anymore. Days are a bit mixing together. I don't remember details about this day. Day 20 We start the day with a step and long climb again. This time through the clouds. A part of the climb was in shingle type rocks, step and exposed. The way up was a little tricky, the way down in party's plain scary. It might also have been the steepest packed dirt descent i have ever done. It took some time. Two more climbs over adjacent passes and we finally saw the Baroude lakes. That day they presented themselves on cloud cover and with strong wind. It did not prevent us from a dip in the lake, drying off was fast. After dinner the wind died down almost completely and I could sleep under the stars, behind a big big rock. As the wind gusts kicked back into gears, I was a little more protected, at 2400m I was comfortable all night in my little sleeping bag. Meeting two dutch people and chatting with them over dinner was really supper interesting. Max and Natalie where hiking companions on our next day trip to Parzan. Day 21 We took an alternate route over a summit and into a scenic valley and hoped for a hitch hike down to Parzan for food supplies. Only 20 min later we were in a camper van on our way. Lunch in the heat of the e valley together was great. Then phone and battery charging, did shopping and we hiked in the baking sun along the road, only for 1.2km, but it was still no fun. The long long climb up the gravel road was much easier to handle with conversations with our new friends. Almost 4h later we arrived at a little reservoir, does and fetched a close camp site. Late dinner together and in the sleeping bag we go. Day 20 We got a good start, early and in a group. We picked up the two Germans along the trail, also just getting started. Super nice day in the morning but warm afternoon. The decent was through trees and not hard at all. We reached the small Refugio Viados at 12.30, before they lunch kitchen opened. After lunch we needed to face the heat on our way up 850m. First hot, then these last 550m up we're super super steep. I felt strong and good. We are spending the night practically at the pass, near a little lake. We need to get out of the Rutt where we climb in the afternoon, somehow. Tomorrow is another day. Day 22 The night was windy and pretty cold, Ryan almost setup a tent, Rosa helped herself to a Bivi sack to get warmer,Gilad seemed to have a cold child night. I only had trouble with the for opening of my sleeping bag and had cold feet as a result. A we are on a GR10 trail, footwork is much easier and we make faster progress. We decendet to the next hour for about 2h and had bad coffee and expensive sandwich before we moved on, re - united with it new German hiking friends, Kilian and Katie from Munich. 7 people in total we matched down towards the bottom of the valley and a restaurant, with great talks and good company. Time flew by and we had lunch pizza and FRESH salad. Amazing. Matt, Kilian and Katie stayed there and got food supplies while we marched in the heat, up to three unmanned Refugio for tonight 800m climb on the road. Mountains are huge again here and colors and patterns simply amazing. We could briefly talk to the Guardia Civil people that just returned from their hiking/climbing training. Impressive people. We meet another Spanish hike at the hut after eating out clothes and ourself. Spanish people are amazing and super friendly and humble. His dogs name is Massai and super cute.... Really. Today we had a cold dinner with our far traveled salame and cheese. Looks like tomorrow might finally be an easier day. Let's see.
