# Our walkway project

*Published: June 01, 2014*

*Original URL: [https://gorunamountain.blogspot.com/2014/05/our-walkway-project.html](https://gorunamountain.blogspot.com/2014/05/our-walkway-project.html)*

---

My sweet live wanted to get a better walkway in the front yard. I finally got my butt of the ground to do it. 

Making the forms after leveling the ground was harder than I thought, but ok. I have to say the concrete business is more of an art than science. Watching the finisher do books magic was pretty humbling. Still want to do it myself next time. Now I just have to find time to finish it up.
