# Facts about water

*Published: July 28, 2008*

*Original URL: [https://gorunamountain.blogspot.com/2008/07/facts-about-water.html](https://gorunamountain.blogspot.com/2008/07/facts-about-water.html)*

---

How much water is used to get our food, drinks on the table is something that reflects the tool it has on our environment. Here are just a few facts from the sprinkla magazine.

EMBODIED WATER OF COMMON FOODS
1 cup of lettuce > 11 litres
1 orange (100g) > 50 litres
1 apple (100g) > 70 litres
1 glass beer (250 ml) > 75 litres
1 glass wine (125 ml) > 120 litres
1 coffee (125 ml) > 140 litres
1 bag of potato crisps (200g) > 185 litres
1 glass milk (200 ml) > 200 litres
1 steak (230g) > 4660 litres
1 kilo of rice > 3400 litres
1 kilo of beef > 16 000 litres

Source: http://www.sprinkla.com/files/Sprinkla_02.pdf
