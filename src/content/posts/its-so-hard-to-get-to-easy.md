I am not really complaining with this post. I am just expressing my amazement. Amazed about the amount of effort it takes to move one's life. The whole life I mean. All the stuff you own, all the things you take for granted being where you are, the insurances, the bank accounts, the car, the supermarket you know, the hardware store you know, the pastry shop you found giving you exactly the type of smile you want on a Saturday after getting up late and you take what you deserve and what you deserve is an easy day with a coffee and a good pastry in the sun, maybe with a friend. Your bicycle supply shop, the bicycle repair guy, the location where you buy motor oil, get your pictures framed, buy your insurance, the place where you can go and ask for help at the city, ..... You know, stuff you need to support real life. Everyday items and locations, people, information. We need so so much, some of it every day, some only once a year. But we do know where to do it and how to do it, we also usually speak the language. For Mumu and me, none of that was true when we started this gigantic project of moving our life to Italy and moved it to a rural area, in that rural area we moved to a pretty remote location.

We now have moved in the house since October and I could not be more grateful. I mean that. It feels so so good to have walls around you that you know will not change. We are located in an area where we can walk out of the gates and be in nature. I have that super high on my list of appreciative things, for sure. Starting to get the legs back into a little bit of shape is needed and feels good, doing that in an environment so beautiful and plentiful as here and as close is super. And my mind is on an easier track now than during March-April of the year. But I have to say, some days I am still in crisis mode and my head does not seem to empty of things I really need to do or get done, EVER. I am not myself in terms of being the open and creative solution finder that I usually am. I have blinds on and miss easy solutions, make things harder than they need to be, ...... I am not in a quiet mind set. YET. I would love to get there soon!!

All this is just to realize how much it takes to truly move one's life. I did not expect this.

In the meantime, we are able to see and discover our area pretty well. We managed to pick one of the coldest days to go up the mountain to see Eremo di Camaldoli again, and it ended up being -5 to -10C up there. COOOLD. We did not bring quiet the right clothing for this, but we just hiked a little faster and we stayed warm(ish). It is rewarding to me seeing nature change in the seasons. In another 4 months it will have been one year we got to experience this place, right here. It s such an interesting mix of personalities here.

I cleared my mountain bike from all the spiderwebs, go the tubeless tires back in working condition and set out to start the process of "**G**etting **M**y **L**egs **B**ack" (GMLB). I know it will take a few months, to catch up again to the state I was in towards the beginning of the year, or even middle of last year. It's humbling and it is still fun. I also need this time by myself for mental recovery. I feel so much better after only 1.5 or 2 hours ride. And riding now means on the mountain bike. The road bike is too cold for me on many days. Then it means to start riding from the house, straight on the trails and into our forest. It is a mud bath, many days and my bike as well as I need a good shower after. COVID is not a real restriction in this regard as I usually meet none on the rides. There is no Police to check, but also really no reason to check as I am just as alone here as in the house.

I made a longer set of pictures which starts from signing the contract to buy the house, going to California, selling the house, see you later to many of our friends, to confinement in Germany and then the process of remodeling the house and land till today. To come back to the original topic of this post, getting to the "easy" way of living. I find it hard and I need to bring out a lot of effort, planning and doing, to finally get to the easy and grounded life I am looking for. I learned to appreciate the planning and action it takes for people that look like they do have an easy life now. Smart decisions will have been made. And what we see is "easy". If anyone found an easy way to EASY, please let me know.
