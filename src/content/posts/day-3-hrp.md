We started out 3rd day later as the distance was smaller and we felt like it. Breakfast including lentils with a spoiler of pickle juice proved to be a true winner. We started on complete fog, and it was absolutely beautiful. We did our first x county segment and it was successful. We managed to stay under the trees most of the time and it was cool. 2.5km before our destination we had lunch. Funks sandwich was great. We strolled down to the town of Aldudes, the bar was closed but we could find our youth hostel. A dip in the river was followed by a set of drinks and food in the "Boutique/Restaurant" of the town. What a good relaxing afternoon.
