Rest day in Salardu with a late wakeup, hotel style breakfast, visit to Vielha where we could buy new socks, gas, t-shirt and more as well as an Asian lunch with delicious beer and good view of town. Dinner with a larger team as Kilian and Kati could join us. Pizza, fresh salad and a little more beer. Great time to reunite as it was our last night with Gilad. He was a joy to hike with, but now he has a plane to catch from Zurich and hitchhiking there takes time. A final bottle of wine in an old place with some outdoor live music. One out of five songs were Spanish, the others were US hits from all different decades.

Day 27 We took the bus from Salardu for the first 4-5km to the ski resort town, Bacquiera/Beret. Therefore our start, after the mandatory slide photos were taken. Not scenic but fast. Once out of the resort the scenery was great and we even got to chat with a group of Spanish friends/hikers. So pleasant. We arrived at the lake JUST as the clouds and fog disappeared. What a show. We/I made a consequential route mistake and we ended up much too far to the right of the peak we where supposed to target. I should have listened to Rosa much more carefully. I will try to not make this mistake again and focus on the group dynamic much more during these decisions. We had to call off an attempt to rock climb our way up to the peak and carefully descended steeply on grass-pebbles down then 1/2h rock hopping to get to something easy and walkable. Short lunch and another 45min to an orange roof Refugio with a great lake. The indoor space filled up rapidly and we had 8 people sleeping outside. A cold night but we got an early start anyway. Great walking in the fresh temperatures. A long but pretty easy and scenic descent to our lunch spot refugio d'Alos with amazing Spanish omlet and Gaspachio. Super nice people and a cute little town. Maybe a bit late in the day but happy we started our 700m ascent in the heat. Beginning was super steep then more shallow in the shade. We finally have horses again. Even on our camp spot. So curious they almost ate or sleeping pad and Rosa's socks drying on a tree branch. Relaxed afternoon with mountain scenery. ==> Happy. 😉

Day 29 We got another early start today. Good as it gave us shadow for almost all our climbing. It is only 6km to our next hut, but it is very rocky and very steep. Routefinding was involved and down climbing reasonably risky. While beautiful scenery was all around, with lakes and amazing rock shapes and colors, the going was very slow and tiring. 5h for 6km. At our little Refugio Pujol, we left or Backpacks and hiked up to a nice view point. The ridge for me, the summit for Rosa and Ryan. Amazing all around view, into France and into Spain. I could see way into the plains of France. Grand view for sure, seeing the difference between Spanish and French Pyrenees. Because of the clouds, I will try again to sleep in the Refugio. So far, almost all the attempts ended with me escaping to the outside anyway. So let's see. Day 30 The night was good and not too warm, none snored. We did our first departure without breakfast. It worked well. 1.5h into the walk we had breakfast next to a Cabana. A gentle descent through pleasant mixed tree forest. Loved it. Our lunch spot was combined with a lake dip, so so rejuvenating. The last climb to the pass went bye quickly, Funk went to the summit Rosa and I bailed on this and went straight to the hut for a beer and camp site search. Apparently the bunk beds in this (Certascan) hut are super tightly spaced. Food and staff were excellent and we had a good time talking to a group of Belgians. There was drinking too. Ryan spent some more time with them and had trouble finding the tent site.... But persistence prevailed.

Day 31 A great night outside. Some rain, lightning and wind, but nothing serious. Apparently we might have to expect similar weather the next 2-3 days. Not bad having a little cool down. We started 7.30 in some clouds and also blue sky. The scenery was superb coming down and looking back at the hut. The reservoir spewed it enormous amounts of water and made noise in accordance with it. A nice little climb to the opposite side of the valley made for great visuals too. As we dove into the next valley, there was silence, all of a sudden. No sound from the water, just silence. The view was stunning into the new lake and valley. What a beauty. Slightly downhill and pleasant. A tiny rock climbing passage to follow before we moved on to the new and bigger valley which demanded long whacky traverses over tall grasses and bushels, through trees. The only positive thing during all this traverse episode were the blueberries left and right on the trail. Tony but sorry tasteful. A little blueberry taste explosion in our mouths. This must have been the lowlight of all traverses I have ever done. Not at all a pleasant walk. This was followed by a step decent through the forest. In parts I felt like in Casentino Valley (my home) when I lost my path. After finishing 1000m descent we finally crossed the river and started our 1000m climb. Fortunately the trail was excellent and stunningly beautiful. We were treated to a lunch spot with waterfall shower. What a super treat. The rest of the ascent was just as comfortable to manage. Just that we saw a young Spanish man walking down really really slowly, with two other men on his side. He had a bandage on his head and leg. They said they do not need any help, so we walked on with even more care and a humbled mind. Don't forget to be careful with every step. We managed to be the first at the hut, only Max was already there, sleeping the whole morning. It is very very windy here, unusual..... Let's see what the next days bring. Ooooh, super surprise. Kilian and Katie arrived at 20.30 at our little hut. They caught me peeing..... It is so nice to see these two again. It looks like the trail provides. Day 32 We left without breakfast again. I like it a lot this way. The scenery in the morning was superb, the lake and the mountains around us. Then came the first group of kids, up the mountain. Probably 25-30 of them with I think teachers. Then another adult hiking group, then a women hiking group,.... Then then, in the end probably more than 50 people walked up to the lake this morning. We are so glad to have been there with only a hand full of hikers. We stopped at the next refugio, 15min out of the way. It was rewarding as they had internet access. Slow, but the first we had in many days. A quick note to Muriel to say I am ok. Then we had to climb the daily ~1000m up. The large valley offered amazingly beautiful views. Then down the pass again traversing to a spectacular valley and huge block of a mountain. Very impressive. After a short walk on a gravel road we are on a trail again and move up the 300m quickly to our lake and camping spot. Cleaning, then dinner with amazing Spanish Jamon. 🍖. An Australian young guy asked if he could join us and we had our usual dish, but with much entertainment. Good evening. Then, at almost 21.00 our two German friends came in. Super nice. The clouds provide an amazing spectacle moving in and around us here. Tomorrow should be a short day, but let's see. Day 33 It was a reasonably short day and we enjoyed it mostly together. A different day for all of us. We climbed up steeply again in the morning before breakfast then descended to a ski resort. There we got our breakfast and had some more internet which was fast enough to call Muriel. Yepeeeeeeeee!! So good to hear her voice and hear what is going on at home and chat a little about what we are going through here. It helps my mind a lot. We took a gondola 🚠 down to a parking lot, then walked the rest of the way on an easy gravel road to El Serat. No shops here, just 3 or so Hotels/Restaurants. I ate 1.5 pizzas and a mixed salad. So so needed it. Then, to top it all off we took a bus a few km up the road to the start of our afternoon treck. We climbed over our pass and I finally figured out what helps me climb better again in some time. Stefan needs more FOOD. I will try to pack more and eat more from now on --> smile even more. We found a nice but pretty cold camp spot near a lake for all our cleaning needs. A social dinner with our German friends was awesome. I am learning about more plants that are useful for us in every day life. Thanks Katie! Day 34 Heading out early as we were high up. We had a nice breakfast at a tiny stream with amazing views. Traversing and climbing and descending as usual. We could spice it up with an amazing swim in an amazing lake with an amazing views next to Cabana Sorda. 5 Stars. We hiked to the next manned hut and called it a day. Well deserved. What a hut. Great atmosphere and great people around, just the breakfast was a little little. Day 35 It is the last day of this segment. A lot of descent over big boulders, first in the nice cold shade, then in the blazing sun. It eased of on the boulders after 2-3h and we could actually walk, not climb only. After a short lunch with a Christian memorial nap, we descended into l'Hospitalet. A big let down from my point of view as it is so small and so un-attractive, only one restaurant, one Gite', and the most popular shop, the bakery. It seems like these are the only people that understand, there is a daily stream of hikers coming that are hungry and thirsty and want to be fed. They are an Ucranian couple and are doing a great great job. Thank you. We had our group dinner here and met another Israeli lady, Rita. She has the warmest and most winning smile I have seen in a long long long time. A nice and warm, see you later and it was finished.
