I want to start off with the main message of this post. My Mom is dead. My Mom is now done with this somewhat absurdity we call life. And I can say, I am glad for her, she now is done with this phase on this planet. It was no longer her type of life, her quality of living and giving.

_**Life**_ is really an absurd construct. We are born, it is hard to learn all these new things, touches, smells, looks, behaviors, movements. We learn about our family, about other people we decide to like, and learn what we can do to please them, upset them, get them to do what we want and so much more. None of this is easy, some things are pleasant, most are not so pleasant. Unless we learn to sprinkle highlights into this thing called life, it will most likely just plot along, on its own terms, we just do what is needed at the time, some are fun, some not so much.

So my Mom is now done with this thing called life. And if I can be the judge, she managed to sprinkle plenty of meaningful parts into these almost 84 years. Truly meaningful, as I am not talking about owning this house or that car, this new toy or that fancy dress. Meaningful for Marita was to have a family that knows the true meaning of family, to have friends that truly deserve being called a friend. It was also to do things no one has really done before, being an ancor and an innovator as well. 

_**Style**_ was big for Marita. She seems to have had style since she became around 18-20 years old. The earlies pictures I see of her being young, she dressed to fashion and took care of herself. I think my Mom was HOT. Her appartement always was maticulously decorated. Not cold, but a style that was lived in and modern. Flower arrangements done so creatively, you could place them in any flower store. The decoration and color combinations were always well tuned and thought through, nothing was left to chance.

_**Sports**_ were always big for Mom and Dad, meeting on a Ski vacation was just the start. It was 1974 where they decided to take my sister and me on a bicycle tour. You know, one with pannier bags on the rack and a map in your hands, having a destination for the day and trying to make it there, rain or shine, with hills or without. None of our friends could tell us which bikes would work best, what clothes to wear, how to be safe on the roads. We met exactly one other group of cyclists in our 1st week of riding. 

Marita also went to a school for Ice climbing after being scared on a Glacier while hiking and did not know what to do to be safe. During class she had to jump into a crevasse and getting pulled back out. It was part of the drill, yes, Marita did it and yes she felt better on the next alpine hiking trip.

_**Skiing**_. It brought Mom and Dad together in Salbach. We did it together as a familyThe bug was implanted in us kids with 5-6 yeas of age. A local park with a little hill is where I started skiing, and I have not stopped since. Summer vacations were kept small to be able to afford a week of skiing.

Marita made herself the communication and care center of the apartment house she lived in for almost 50 years. She gave life meaning by helping others in the house through some rough times, or just to sit down and having a casual and community-building chat with them. That could be almost any time of the day and as long as it was needed to get the other person in a better state of mind.

The Nordkapp, Australia, California, were some of the remote travel destinations of her. But it did not take distance for Marita to create meaning and substance in life. A simple walk or bike ride could evolve into something you might never forget with her. She did anything and everything for others, she directed her decisions to what others would like more and did that with fun and passion. There was always a friend that needed some special time of her, some extra words, some certainty of love and she was always there to deliver.

She taught me so much, she shaped me and guided me. I think she knows how grateful I was and am.

I am still in awe and amazement about the way Marita dealt with the news of her Tumor last March. But really, I am more amazed how she dealt with her own experience of aging and capabilities being removed from her tool belt. She fought many of them with fervor and unrelenting attitude. She fought others with grace, on her own terms. I am certain she was nearly 100% aware of what this Tumor does to her and at what speed. She almost never talked about it though. Any form of negativity around her would not be acceptable. Positive vibes and a good outlook on life would continue. She seemed to manage to create so much of a positive bubble around her, she was truly a little surprised when we had to break certain facts to her about her true state of health. She seemed shocked how we saw her and what we felt are the upcoming steps. She was smart, and focussed inwardly in this regard, not letting the devastating news affect her outlook on life. Giving up riding her newly acquired bicycle just like driving a car was never a done deal, it was just not in the cards at this point in life. Death as a concept could be discussed to a certain degree, but she would only contemplate her own state in her own head. Not shutting us out, but dealing with it as a truly private matter and discussing matters only on an “_as needed_” basis.

_**She is done**_ with this strange thing called life, I think she was happy to call it an end, finding herself in the conditions she was. She could and should have rocked our world for another few years. I would have enjoyed another few sessions of joy with her, of fun with her, a few sessions of enjoyment with her.
