We have submitted an offer and it was accepted with some conditions and tweaks. It is a long process with many possibilities of falling through, but it is another step in the right direction. In the direction of us 2 settling in the Casentino Valley. I will NOT praise the night before the FAT lady sings. But I will say we both are very happy about where we are. Let the project begin.

In the meantime, Mumu is here with me and the French Family arrived here too for a week of vacation before we go and check out Sicily for 3 weeks.

Last week I prepared for a group ride up Pratomagno, one of the highest mountains h=in the area. Mumu wanted to ride up and our hosts, Lucrezia and Francesco joined in as well. I rode up the day before to check out the best roads and see how far the "E-Bike" crew will make it in this area. It was smart to do that as some roads were pretty rough on the gravel side and the route had way more intensity that I think would have been appreciated by the team. 1st lap done on this climb, Thursday, all 4 of us went out on Friday with 3 E-Bikes and my "S" drive (Stefan drive). I was LAST on the climbing for a change this time. We took our time with several stops to look at houses and to have a little snack along the way in Quora. Lucrezia managed to run out of E-Bike battery 1km before the summit. The price you pay for passing us all in Turbo mode every time. Mumu and I managed to get to the Cross of Pratomagno, then back down the same way we came. It is an impressive view from up there, we were chased back down by the looming clouds and thunder around us. The best thing was that Mumu really enjoyed the ride, and it might be a great way to do these things together.

And what a great day to send together with our hosts since more than 5 weeks this year.
