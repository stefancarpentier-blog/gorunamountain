## TransAlp

So I did this thing called TransAlp in August. A big word for some, meaning nothing to most. For me I saw it as a vehicle to get something done in my head. After 2 years of remodeling and reconstruction projects of our new home in Tuscany my brain was tired. Really tired and it showed effects on how I react to situations and interact with others. I decided in the spring,

_Stefan needs a reset, re-callibration, mental grounding,.... I needed some real time off_.

I wanted to be in nature, still with people. I wanted to shut up for a while, while still being around people. I wanted to be alone, but not all alone. I wanted to do something, without having to plan a whole lot, without having to organize stuff. I wanted a simple time. Simple for me, predictable to a certain degree, enough chances for stuff to go wrong a little bit, so I can get myself back out of it. --> So I decided to put one foot in front of the other and do that for a while, do that for a long while. As I have done plenty of Trans Sierra trips in California, a TransAlp seemed appropriate. For the not so athletically inspired, a TransAlp is a crossing of the Alps by the strength of your own body. You can do it any way you want, by foot, by bike, on skies, paragliding, hang-gliding, .... or any other sport, or mix between them. I had the idea of doing this crossing the Alps as well as attaching a short 6 day hike from Bologna back home to it, for good measure.

## **Planning**

Where do I cross, which path do I take, which huts do I go to, how high do I want to be, how difficult do I want this thing to be, and much could be decided ahead of time, the route nailed down, saved on my phone and I would have a pretty simple life for a while. Well, I actually could attach a segment in the Apennine mountain range tot he end of this TransAlp and walk right back home. Done deal. I have a rough plan. Who is coming? Well, who wants to do such a thing AND can take off more than one month for this AND can handle me for all this time and not smack me in the face every day for being so stupid to go on a hike like this? WHO? Well I was lucky and found two fabulous people, Ralf and Manuela. Both with the right attitude to such an endeavor and the body to do it. The logistics were figured out pretty quickly and the trip was ready to start. Right after Cassandre's and Gabri's wedding in London we took off. July 28, Thursday, 7.20 the train left Nuernberg with Ralf and me. We picked up Manuela in Allersberg and were in Munich only 1 1/2 hours later. A short S-Bahn ride later we were at our starting point just north of Lenggries, about 60km south of Munich. A brief lunch at the "Brezelstuben" and we were off on our trip. The destination for the 1st part of this trip was Lago di Garda in Italy, not knowing what would happen along the way and how things would change. I read from other people and other adventures; plan your trip, read about it, give yourself options along the way, know your route, ..... Then, once it starts, be ready to change it all the 1st day.

## No, Yes, Maybe, Why

I don't want to write a whole recap of the trip here. Maybe I do that later. I want to write about what happened during the trip. I have never done a trip doing biking, riding or otherwise for such a long time. I was pretty certain my legs and rest of the body would be up for this. I was not so certain how it would work with my head. I wanted to calm this thing down, I wanted to come out the other side of this trip better than I went in. People told me all kinds of things.

- After one week your body is used to it and all will be fine.

- Are you crazy, why would you do that to yourself.

- I would get bored after 2-3 days.

- You will be so relaxed after this, I want to go too.

- What if X,Y,Z happens....

And of course, it all came totally different. I had a terrible 1st night. My belly rebelled, I could not sleep, I had a screaming headache ( I never have headaches). As a result I felt terrible the 1st day and had to nurse myself through the day. I was able to hike the 2nd day. I was weak, needed to cough, could not eat, barely drank anything. Next night was good, but the following day barely better. ==> Doubts came up in my mind. Are you really up for this? Why are you doing this? Day 3 was much better but I was still far from being myself. In the meantime, it rained every day, from "not much" up to "2-3 hours and really hard". That did not help me, but I muddled through and on day 5 or so, I was able to walk at a speed uphill again that I knew I could and I wanted to. I was still not at 100% but the body felt good enough to proceed. This "unproductive cough" stuck with me till the very end of this adventure.

One short statistic. We hiked ~420km and climbed ~20.000m in the process. It took us 29 days to do that.

## What happened in that head?

But something happened during this time. I could see a certain rhythm in our days and it was comforting. We get up at 6.30, start hiking at 7.30 or 8.00 latest. Then we hike till about 14.00 to 17.00 at the longest and where done. Some days it would be a little tricky to find the route, some days we had more technical terrain, some others we would have to climb up steep hills just to go back down the other side, some days it would rain a little more, some others it would be warm. At the end of the day, we would arrive in one piece, in a good mood and nicely tiered, between 14.00 and 17.00 in the afternoon, would eat some cake and have a beer at the hut, eat dinner, chat with fellow hikers or not and go to bed. The next morning all this would repeat. We all stopped worrying about the details of the route of the day, will it be too hard for us, will we make it over this pass, or that decent. It wold all work out till the afternoon and it would be good. This was a pretty neat rhythm. What of this could or would I want to transfer to my regular life? How can I enter every day now, as i am back home and be able to be as happy and content with every evening, going to bed nicely tired, not worrying about much. Then I only worried about if my bed neighbor will be snoring that night (earplugs or not).

## Take something home, Stefan

I want to take some part "home" from this. What can I learn from this?

At least in concept, I want to be able to take this contentness and easy going mind set with me. I think it could help me be happier, lead a little lighter life.

* * *

I tend to think and optimize things a lot, try to predict what all could come from doing it this way vs. that way. I am not a worrier, but I think about the impact my actions have on others, how others actions might impact me a lot. Maybe some of this can be reduced. Maybe, just maybe.

**Others**: I try to influence people I care about. I try to take an interest in the direction they seem to take, ..... All this, while it is well meant, and it got me to the state in my life is taking a toll on me. All this takes a lot of effort on my side and I have to say, only very few things I stated to others actually resulted in a directional change from them. It was just that I cared so much about them. Why would I not say something, at the right time, the right way...... I did not do that during the 4 weeks and life went on.  
I only speak this out because I care, it only gets ignored because others don't usually want to hear it, don't want to talk about it or what ever other reason.

**Me**: I can't say that I care a huge amount what others think of me. I do, but I don't really. I think I want to take this attitude of easy going, accepting what comes, seeing a top level rhythm in my days weeks and months. That does not seem that easy to me. Not easy to get to easy.

1st step: No-one shall get my well meant advice, and probably, no-one will notice.

2nd step: I have to think about that one, but I will start with a nightly listing of all the good/bad things that happened to me that day, how it is similar to the day before, and not think what I might do tomorrow. Maybe I can find this pattern in my days, maybe I can trust in the flow there is and just go with it. Go with the flow and see where it is taking me. There is so so so much talk about that going around.

- let the force be with you

- follow the flow

- see where life carries you

- when you'r falling - dive

Many concepts that seem quiet similar.

I am happy I did this, I think it worked out for me, I can only highly recommend it to anyone that needs to clear the head. Choose what seems right for you, don't copy what I or someone else did.
