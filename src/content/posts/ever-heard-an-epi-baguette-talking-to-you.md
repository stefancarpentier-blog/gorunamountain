Over the last 1 year or so, I went down the path of learning how to make French style Baguette. The fact that I hang out around French people a lot, at work as well as private I think helped motivate me. Living in California with an upcoming trend to put value in what you eat and drink more and more certainly pushed me along.

### "Ba**guette**" – which literally means "**a stick**" in French.

I started with a recipe from the web, one that seemed to make sense. The **1st** version was OK and had a clear resemblance of a Parisian baguette. After changing the flour,  the yeast, the oven setup and temperature, adding water steam at the beginning, changing the duration, then how to put flour on the dough before you put it in the oven, change the time in the oven,  the shaping and scoring ..... I am now where I am. I am really happy with the results. They come out consistently good. What a process, how fun it is to iterate and discover something new.
