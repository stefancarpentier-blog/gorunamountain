It's been a while since a song captured my heart and mind so deeply. Depending on my state of mind, different lines touch me the most; I find new meaning in lines of the lyrics I already know. It always puts my mind at ease and makes me smile a lot.

When listening to this, I get a good amount of distance from this current world we live in, from the news I receive, from the actions I see, and the responses I need to witness. It gives me a pathway out of this reality of violence, selfishness, shortsightedness, and the singular focus on Money, Power, and influence over others.

The rules in society we used to have seemed to me, at least, established. These rules seem to be followed very "_selectively_" nowadays; These rules now seem to be all but absent, thoroughly ignored, and are in the process of being eliminated. This song and the lyrics guide me to a mental place I focus on. I focus on what **I CAN** influence, and focus on the world **I WANT TO** live in.

This is not a call to action, just a moment for me to possibly connect with you. I only want to share what is going through my mind right now. Maybe to put you at ease, that you might not be the only one struggling with the rapid developments of the last year or more.
