I will teach. Teach others. Teach them how to make simple silver rings. I am so excited to start this, and somewhere in my head there is the hope I start a new phase of my life. I know I love teaching others. I did it in High Tech. Now I want to do it with much more hands on subjects. The result will actually exist in real life, not on a monitor screen only but in someone's hand, on someone's finger. And with a little luck people will have fun learning and doing it, maybe the results will look good too.
