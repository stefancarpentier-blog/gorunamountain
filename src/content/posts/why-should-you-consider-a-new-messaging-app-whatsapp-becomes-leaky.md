Jan Koum, the founder of WhatsApp is originally from the Soviet Union. This might explain why he feels the need for privacy of communication, not for any other reason that you don't want to share everything you say and do with everyone. I think that is a good policy. The fact that he now left Facebook concerns me enough about my privacy that I want to drop WhatsApp and start using e.g. Threema. A truly private messaging app.
