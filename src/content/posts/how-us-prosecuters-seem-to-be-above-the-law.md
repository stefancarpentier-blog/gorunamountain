The way I read this in brief;

- A man is held accountable for a murder he did not commit
- He is held in prison for 18 years,
- Is on Death row for 14 years of this
- The prosecutor of this case knowingly and intentionally withholds evidence of the case that shows him not being guilty (DNA), violating the "Brady Rule"
- The man is set free.
- The man who lost 18 years of his life in prison proves that the US prosecutor did all this just to get a conviction, not to find out the truth and hold him accountable for this act.
- The case goes to the US Supreme court and gets turned down, leaving the prosecutor in office and free to do the same life destructive thing today and tomorrow again.

“I don’t care about the money,” he wrote in an Op-Ed article in The [New York Times](http://www.nytimes.com/2011/04/10/opinion/10thompson.html "Op-Ed article") in 2011. “I just want to know why the prosecutors who hid evidence, sent me to prison for something I didn’t do and nearly had me killed are not in jail themselves.
