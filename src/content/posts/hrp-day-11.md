After a good meal and am exciting game of Mikado, we went to bed. Some outside, some inside. After 4h sleeping inside, I woke up and it was so hot, so bad air in our tiny room, I had to escape and went outside with my sleeping bag. Super. We went through our morning wakeup and stretching routine and opted for an alternate route, including a summit. It worked out that the climb we choose was much more technical than anticipated, but we made it up safely, not so comfortable. It took about 2h out of our day and we got into the late afternoon heat. I suffered a lot with it and with the help of salted peanuts and special magnesium I recovered and we made it to the ski town of Candancu'. Incredible scenery and sorry colored mountain sides I will not forget. Again, to much food for dinner and it tastes very good. We are being tested like queen and king here food wise. Tomorrow we will be off to Pombie hut. It should be super scenic. Stefan
