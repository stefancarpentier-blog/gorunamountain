# USGBC (United States Green Building Council)

*Published: July 09, 2008*

*Original URL: [https://gorunamountain.blogspot.com/2008/07/usgbc-united-states-green-building.html](https://gorunamountain.blogspot.com/2008/07/usgbc-united-states-green-building.html)*

---

Today was my 1st real appearance at a USGBC event. It was  a monthly meeting between construction professionals that are dedicated and knowledgeable to reconstruct and build in a sustainable and environmentally conscious way. The added benefit is of course that you get to live or work in an environment that is not hazardous to your health. It was also the very 1st time I actually met Sushanta in person. Sushanta  is a Interior designer and already very knowledgeable in the field. She is running a student education program that I find amazing. But please, read about it yourself. www.empoweringworldchange.com
The Northern California Chapter is the one I will join to learn more about LEED and how to get certified. http://www.usgbc-ncc.org/
