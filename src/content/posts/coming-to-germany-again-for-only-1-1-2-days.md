The initial investigation and property finding in our Casentino area is now over, or may I say, interrupted?! We went to Nuernberg with the train from Florence; FAST to Bologna, just north of the Apennines, then with a more local and slow train through the flat area of the Po Delta and up and over the Alps. The Brenner Pass is not all that impressive, but it still makes for a nice train journey to be able to look and appreciate all the details by train. The Alps seem structured very very different than the California Sierra Nevada. More pointy, snow much earlier in elevation and still so so much green everywhere in the middle of June. We arrived in Nbg Hbf just after Midnight. My Mom picked us up, right at the gate. I got an awesome Mom, I tell you!! Mumu dropped to bed right after a shower, it took me till 2.45 to hit that sack. So much to catch up on with my Mom. In the morning I got to go to my local bakery to get freshly made, hand made bread rolls that are to die for. When I lived there, I used to be served by Mother Boehmer, since 15-20 years her daughter and husband took over and run the place is a very similar personal way. I love this place. Many new “so-called bakeries” popped up left and right of her. I was a little worried at the time. But the quality and a stubborn clientele of loyalists made her survive and thrive. If you watch and compare the different Bakeries on this street at 7-8.00 in the morning, you know who is the BOSS in that area. At least a line of 5 people are in the shop, sometimes the line goes right out on the street. Did I mention that I like to go there? I managed to get some German administrative stuff done and tick off some items on the list of “_Places to visit when I am home_”. We got to see my sister and my nice Lena, Anna was busy with vacation preparations. I will catch you in two weeks. Another breakfast this morning that deserves the name. Mom loves her big breakfasts and everyone is invited to join in. Cheese, charcuterie, yogurt, fresh fruit different bread, coffee, tea, can I bring you something else? So now, after we dropped a good amount of luggage at her apartment, we are off to Strasbourg, meeting friends and friends of friends. I never visited Strasbourg and am looking forward to seeing this smallish town at the southern end of Alsace. The plan is to do some hiking in the area. Supposedly an amazingly beautiful area. Let’s see. We will follow this up with the more cultural approach of a city visit. I think Mumu has planned for Museums and some of the CHOICE Restaurants in the area. What do the French know about food anyway?!?!?!
