How can this become a top level political item to discuss?

What does it say when the president of the most powerful nation and some politicians start voicing opinions on this matter?

This is a legal issue that needs to be handled by lawyers and judges; no politician can possibly have any influence in the process or outcome of this charge. If we do this type of thing we are no longer bound by law and order. Let's think what to do here and do nothing but let the process take it's course.

Navy SEALs Were Warned Against Reporting Their Chief for War Crimes [https://nyti.ms/2Vfi3XP](https://nyti.ms/2Vfi3XP)

\=====================

Stabbing a defenseless teenage captive to death. Picking off a school-age girl and an old man from a sniper’s roost. Indiscriminately spraying neighborhoods with rockets and machine-gun fire.

Navy SEAL commandos from Team 7’s Alpha Platoon said they had seen their highly decorated platoon chief commit shocking acts in Iraq. And they had spoken up, repeatedly. But their frustration grew as months passed and they saw no sign of official action.
