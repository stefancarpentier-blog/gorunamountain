I signed up to Blinkist, an Audible book APP that condenses non-fiction books

![ORWELL1984](/photos/orwell1984.png)

to its essence and saves me a good amount of time and offers me a valuable service.

I recently read/listened to "ORWL 1984" . A book that was published 1994.  I thought of this book to be so bad, the message outdated and uninformed that I wrote the service.

This is what I wrote.

* * *

I just finished this short summary and have to say this is the most short sighted and not current summary of yours I have heard so far. I don't know if it is there book itself or your summary but at a minimum it is not up to the current knowledge and current starter in many countries. I would invite a review of this contribution.

If you want I can give a large number of examples to my opinion.

Thanks Stefan

* * *

**Response:**

May 3, 12:40 CEST

Hi Stefan,

Thanks for writing in your feedback.

When blinking a book we have decided to stick with the author's opinion stated in the book, even when it contains some contentious claims. I understand that it might be frustrating for some very committed readers with a lot of specialized knowledge, but we try to choose the books featured in our app very carefully and on strong grounds. Please note that the lack of currency in the book's content might be easily explained with the fact that the book was released in 1994.

Since the idea of Blinkist is to offer the big ideas from non-fiction books in a compact format, most important thing for us is to stay true to the original book. I hope you understand :)

Feel free to contact us again if you should have any other suggestions or questions.

All the best, Gi..........

* * *

**My response:**

Thanks for your response. I appreciate it.

I understand the release date hinders the author of "current" knowledge. YOU, however, as Blinkist, seem to carry a responsibility in the release of books as well, just by which books you offer to your current clients. In my view, this very book about Orwell 1984, and it's conclusions are very out of date and lead to conclusions that were correct at the time, best case, un-informed and without significant foresight more realistically.

**YOUR** selection to place this book on your Blinkist Platform and make this obviously outdated information easily available to a large number of people is what I see as irresponsible.

It brings up concerns in my mind how much I should trust your selection process and truly reduces the value Blinkist brings to me.

I value what you do, condensing non-fiction books into the essence. I expect the information presented to me to be current. I understand the topic of this very book well and could reject the outdated content. I would like to convince Blinkist to protect me from wrong, outdated information in areas where I am not as informed.

Am I expecting something unreasonable?

Thanks for your time.

Stefan
