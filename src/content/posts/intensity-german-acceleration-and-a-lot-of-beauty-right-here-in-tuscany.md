In all this whirlwind of impressions, rushing here for a house viewing, driving there to check out a new village, coming back for a visit with a contractor, saying thanks with some homemade bread..... Here in Tuscany there always seems enough time for a great experience.

**Eating a salad**, with stuff we bought in just a simple market brings out flavors that I almost forgot. Thin Fennel slices that have an intensity of taste, chicory leaves that have no problem standing out in the flavor profile, green lettuce that is actually crunchy, olive oil that actually tastes like olives. These are not so subtle reminders of what food can taste like if it is grown well, harvested when it is ripe and brought to the market the next day for you to buy.

**Eating meat**, prepared with just salt and some herbs, grilled to perfection. The portions that we got did not breach the side of our plates but were just as much as I could or wanted to eat. The juices of this orchestra were few but delicious. The Restaurant was 3-4km out of Arrezo in the hills. Unassuming and family owned since 50+ years. The service was personal, fast and heartfelt. There was nothing even remotely fake about this afternoon.

**The drive** there was exhilarating as we were driven by our newly made friends  Roberto and Grazia. I never sat in a Porche SUV, and I certainly now understand that some automatic transmissions are made for a truly sportive drive and is made in perfection. A safe and smooth ride thanks to some German engineering greatness.

**Listening.** I don't really understand Italian yet. But you don't really have to, to feel the intensity and experience the way communication seems to be done here. There never is a business only conversation, as it seems to me. There is always time for a heartfelt chit-chat and seeing how the other is really doing. Talking with your hands is mandatory as it seems, talking quietly seems like an insult. Emotions are always a big part of communication and you feel it.
