  CAPS 2026

  After the **HRP 2024** , I kinda fell in love with the Pyrenees, more than already and over the next year decided another significant time, maybe another crossing through this beautiful mountain range was in order for me. Contemplating ideas and possible scenarios, I ended up with CAPS.   Coast to Coast Across the Pyrenees, Southern route. This time by bike.  As I could not find any premade routes that fill my bill of so many aspects (length, amount of climbing, beauty of the route, accommodation, northern vs. southern Pyrenees….. I had to come up with one myself. Not as easy as it sounds, plenty opportunities of small and big mistakes. In the end I think I managed to avoid many, still made a few of them. Finding the right team of capable as well as a little foolish was another, finding the time and managing all the rest of the details……

## DAY 4
AUG 18, 2026

  <iframe src="https://ridewithgps.com/embeds?type=trip&id=406169816&metricUnits=true&sampleGraph=true" style="width: 1px; min-width: 100%; height: 700px; border: none;" scrolling="no"></iframe>

― Isaba 2 Yaca ---— Detour around the forest fire

The morning started with an excellent Breakfast from a friendly Baker lady, serving pain o chocolate, croissants and some cake. Truly excellent. But the 7 of us almost cleaned out her store,  several of the local clients in line walked away without their breakfast treat…. Happy but still sad. We continued a little bit down the beautiful valley and turned off to an even less populated street. Finally a pass that was not above 10% . My legs applauded. The way down we managed to lose our downhill rocketeer Tom as he raced past a crucial intersection. For him that meant to climbing back up and meet us in Argo in the region on Aragon (yes like in **Lord of the Rings**). We found a truly amazing deli which fuled is with pizza like flat bread and delicious sweets. Following this, with really heating up conditions, we ran a speed train down the valley towards Yaca. Managed to squeeze in a river dip, and Kim somehow was handed a bottle of water and energy drink by a complete stranger, nice and cold. People are amazing. The “last little stretch" to jaca proved more adventurous. The only real street to get us there was closed for fire danger, the Autobahn being out of question we made our way slowly through remote ranch style houses and gravel roads to Jaca. The campground told is initially, no no no FULL. **_NO MORE_**.  A few minutes and many words later she guided is to a small area. Not pretty, but doable. It was hot, we wanted to stop… I think it was clear to the camp host. The next visitors, asking for a camining spot were plain out rejected. So lucky for us. We chatted up a dutch guy on motorcycle and another one from Catalonia. Warm good people. Dinner and then a thunderstorm and rain that warranted putting up the tent again for sure. What a light show.

## DAY 5
AUG 20, 2026

Yaca 2 Broto 2 Waterfall – Wild camping

The first part of the ride was almost exclusively on main roads.  I wanted to stick as north as possible to the Pyrenees, so today we needed to take a bigger road to go east. As soon as we could, we took a gravel road instead, just a little further North. Some parts we doubted Komoots way to decide on what makes a ridable gravel road. One of the dry river crossings would definetely be impossible to cross with any water. Finally in the town of Gavin, we found an open Restaurant. This happened to be the 2nd of Spanish Restaurants that rejected us, clearly, not because of what was claimed, a lack of table space for us, but most likely because of our appearance. This brought down the "Hospitality reputaion" of the Spanish people we encountered on this and the last trip, from Impeckable to flawed in some spaces. Running on our well espablished "erica energy balls" or other reserves, we started pedaling up this canyon that got tighter with every kilometer we rode. Not steep at all, very pleasant to ride and nicely in the shade. We made it down to Broto, had some food, some drinks but decided that a few more km would benefit our quality of overnight camping. And tru it was as we ended up close to a little waterfall (Dip) on a big and wide meadow. The MOTHER OF THUNDERSTORMS came down to us that night. Yes, heavy rain, butthe lightshow and audio was truly intimidating. We felt very much safe ina valley and away from trees, but we wall were impressed.

  Plane 2 Perpignan an Beach

Leaving this welcoming, warm and beautiful place was made only harder because of the cute and gorgeous Husky they had on the property.
HUSKY IMAGE

We got up early and started breakfast already at 7.00. still out from us till 8.20 to get rolling and started. We opted for the adventurous route done hill, avoiding a 200+m climb up to the highway. It was a fun ride/home down a really really step hill, we learned what these bikes can and cannot do. Ending up at a thermal bath we refilled with water and Rosa and Fabian took off to get their bike box for the plane. We took it a little easier and had a bike design store break and found really nice local streets to avoid the main highway which was not too pretty and reasonably busy. Then also Kim and I broke of from July and Tom and found a nice small parallel street almost all the way into Perpignan. Not hard. not hot. not pretty. We meet up with Rosa and Fabian, and headed for the ultimate goal. The Mediterranean beach. Another 15km and a few wrong turns and we where the. Down with the t shirt, into the water we go.**
