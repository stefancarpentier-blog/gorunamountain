I just watched a movie.

Watching the storyline and action and actors one phrase kicked me right in the face.

### But the trees don't care.

In the current state of affairs in this country, in this world, in our city, .... There is so much that deeply troubles me and I feel helpless and out of control. I want to post, I want to scream and I want to act. But the small wheel I am, I don't seem to see how and when I could make anything point into a more pressing direction for me. I can live my life in my little bubble and try to make my world pleasing to myself and my friends and people around me. Maybe a more, don't care, attitude will work better for me. More like the cow the pig the grass the tree.

### But the cows don't care.

### But the pigs don't care.

### But the grass don't care.
